## Which EIN routes are open to a foreign owner with no SSN or ITIN?

Three, and the online application is not one of them.

The [Instructions for Form SS-4](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iss4) (Rev. December 2025) state that an applicant "must have a valid taxpayer identification number (SSN, EIN, or ITIN) in order to use the online application," and that an applicant with no legal residence or principal place of business in the United States "can't use the online application to obtain an EIN."

A foreign person with no U.S. tax identification number, owning a U.S. LLC, is usually caught by both conditions at once. That leaves the telephone, fax and mail routes.

| Route | Who it is for | What the SS-4 instructions say | Practical note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Online** | Applicants with a valid SSN, EIN or ITIN and a U.S. legal residence or principal place of business | Closed to applicants with no U.S. legal residence or principal place of business | Not available to most foreign owners |
| **Telephone** | International applicants only | Call **267-941-1099** (not a toll-free number), 6:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. Eastern time, Monday through Friday, to obtain an EIN | The caller must be authorised to receive the EIN and answer questions about Form SS-4 |
| **Fax** | Any applicant filing Form SS-4 | "Under the Fax-TIN program, you can receive your EIN by fax generally within 4 business days" | No U.S. address: fax **855-215-1627** from inside the U.S., or **304-707-9471** from outside |
| **Mail** | Any applicant filing Form SS-4 | "You will receive your EIN in the mail in approximately 4 weeks"; apply at least 4 to 5 weeks before you need it | No U.S. address: Internal Revenue Service, Attn: EIN International Operation, Cincinnati, OH 45999 |

The telephone route is the one foreign owners most often do not know exists, because the IRS withdrew phone issuance for domestic applicants. The SS-4 instructions describe the call as the way an international applicant obtains an EIN, and state no waiting period for it. Have a completed Form SS-4 in front of you before dialling — the instructions require the caller to be able to answer questions about the form.

## What do the Form 5472 instructions say about a missing EIN?

Nothing. That absence is worth stating plainly rather than filling with a guess.

The [Instructions for Form 5472](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i5472) set out the filing requirement for a foreign-owned U.S. disregarded entity, the "Foreign-owned U.S. DE" notation, the Ogden PIN Unit fax number and address, and the extension route via Form 7004. The instructions do not address whether the DE must have an EIN before filing, or what to enter if it does not have one. The EIN discussion in those instructions concerns 25% foreign shareholders and related parties, not the filer's own missing number.

So any claim about what the IRS "accepts" in the DE's EIN box on a Form 5472 is inference, not instruction. Our [EIN pending at the Form 5472 deadline](/blog/form-5472-ein-pending-deadline) guide works through that gap in more detail.

## Can you file Form 7004 without an EIN?

This is where a pending EIN becomes expensive, because an extension that is not valid is worth nothing.

The [Form 7004 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i7004) direct the filer to "Enter your applicable employer identification number (EIN) or social security number (SSN)" and then warn that if the name entered on Form 7004 does not match the IRS database, or the identifying number is incorrect, you will not have a valid extension. The Form 7004 instructions do not describe an "Applied For" option.

The general guidance about applying for a number appears elsewhere. The SS-4 instructions say that if you do not have an EIN by the time a return is due, "write 'Applied For' and the date you applied in the space shown for the number." That is guidance for a return, written in the SS-4 instructions, and it is not repeated in the Form 7004 instructions or in the Form 5472 instructions for a foreign-owned U.S. DE.

Treat the difference as a risk, not a loophole: a Form 7004 whose identifying number cannot be matched is, on its own instructions, an invalid extension. The safe sequence is to get the EIN first and the extension second, which is why the EIN application should go out the day the problem is discovered rather than the week of the deadline. Our [line-by-line Form 7004 guide](/blog/form-7004-line-by-line-foreign-owned-llc) covers every other entry on the form.

## Should the owner's SSN or ITIN go in the LLC's EIN box?

No. The SS-4 instructions say directly: "Don't show your SSN as an EIN on returns."

The LLC needs its own EIN. The owner's personal number — an ITIN, a foreign tax number, or an SSN if one exists — identifies the owner, not the entity, and substituting one for the other creates a mismatch of exactly the kind the Form 7004 instructions warn invalidates an extension. A foreign owner also does not need an ITIN in order for the LLC to obtain an EIN; the Form SS-4 route for international applicants exists for that reason.

## What should you do in the last weeks before the deadline?

A sequence, in this order:

1. **Confirm no EIN already exists.** An EIN obtained at formation by a registration agent, or applied for and forgotten, is common. If an EIN was issued but the letter is lost, the replacement route is different from a fresh application.
2. **Complete Form SS-4 accurately today.** Name exactly as registered, responsible party, and the reason for applying. See our [Form SS-4 walkthrough for foreign-owned LLCs](/blog/how-to-fill-out-form-ss-4-foreign-owned-llc).
3. **Pick the fastest route you can actually execute.** The international telephone line if the authorised person can call during 6:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. Eastern, Monday to Friday; otherwise fax, which the SS-4 instructions put at generally four business days. Mail at roughly four weeks is a last resort when the deadline is close.
4. **Do not wait for the EIN to start the return.** Reconcile the related-party transactions, assemble the records and prepare the Form 5472 and pro forma Form 1120 while the application is in flight. The number is the last field to fill.
5. **Decide on the extension once the EIN is in hand**, and file Form 7004 by the regular due date if the package will not be ready.
6. **Keep the evidence** — the EIN confirmation, the application date, the fax transmission report — because the dates are the facts any later reasonable cause argument rests on.

## Does a pending EIN extend the Form 5472 deadline?

No. Nothing in the Form 5472 instructions makes the filing obligation or its due date conditional on the EIN arriving. The due date follows the pro forma Form 1120: the [Instructions for Form 1120](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1120) set it at the 15th day of the 4th month after the end of the tax year, moving to the next business day when it falls on a weekend or legal holiday.

An EIN application is an application. It is not an extension, and it is not a defence. What it can be is a documented, dated step showing ordinary business care — which matters if the filing does end up late and the facts have to be written down.

## The bottom line

A foreign owner with no SSN or ITIN has three EIN routes: the international telephone line at 267-941-1099, fax at generally four business days, and mail at roughly four weeks. The online application is closed. Apply now rather than at the deadline, because the Form 7004 extension that would buy you time depends on an identifying number the IRS can match — and a mismatch means no valid extension at all. ## Frequently asked questions

### Can a foreign owner get an EIN without an SSN or ITIN?

Yes. The Form SS-4 instructions provide telephone, fax and mail routes, and give international applicants the number 267-941-1099. Only the online application requires a valid SSN, EIN or ITIN.

### How long does an EIN take for an international applicant?

The SS-4 instructions state that the Fax-TIN program generally returns an EIN within 4 business days, and that mail applications arrive in approximately 4 weeks. They advise applying at least 4 to 5 weeks before you need the number.

### Can I file Form 5472 without an EIN?

The Form 5472 instructions do not address a missing EIN for the disregarded entity. Because the filing attaches to a pro forma Form 1120 identified by that number, obtain the EIN rather than relying on an inference.

### Can I write "Applied For" instead of an EIN?

The SS-4 instructions give that general guidance for a return when the EIN has not arrived. The Form 7004 and Form 5472 instructions do not repeat it, and Form 7004 warns that an unmatched number means no valid extension.

### Can I use my ITIN as the LLC's EIN?

No. The SS-4 instructions say not to show your SSN as an EIN on returns, and the same logic applies to an ITIN. The LLC needs its own EIN.

### Does applying for an EIN extend the Form 5472 deadline?

No. The due date follows the pro forma Form 1120 and does not depend on when the EIN arrives. Only a valid, timely Form 7004 extends the filing deadline.

### Which fax number does a foreign applicant use for Form SS-4?

The SS-4 instructions direct applicants with no legal residence or principal place of business in a U.S. state to fax 855-215-1627 from within the United States, or 304-707-9471 from outside it.
