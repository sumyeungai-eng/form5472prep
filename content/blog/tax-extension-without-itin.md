**You do not need an ITIN to request an extension of time to file a federal individual income tax return or to make an estimated tax payment. Use the applicable paper form or payment voucher, write “ITIN TO BE REQUESTED” where it asks for an SSN or ITIN, and do not attach Form W-7. An extension gives you more time to file; it does not give you more time to pay tax due.**

This matters when a nonresident may need a personal U.S. return but cannot assemble the W-7 package before the filing deadline.

## Which action should you take before an ITIN exists?

Keep the extension, payment, and ITIN application in separate lanes.

| Your immediate job | Document or route | What goes in the TIN field? | Does Form W-7 go with it? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Request more time to file Form 1040, 1040-SR, 1040-NR, or 1040-SS | Paper Form 4868 | “ITIN TO BE REQUESTED” | No |
| Make an estimated individual payment | Form 1040-ES or 1040-ES (NR) voucher with check or money order | “ITIN TO BE REQUESTED” | No |
| File the federal return that establishes the ITIN need | Form W-7 application package with the return, unless a listed exception applies | First-time applicant: leave the applicant's TIN area blank; renewal: include the existing ITIN | Yes |

The [IRS ITIN page](https://www.irs.gov/tin/itin/individual-taxpayer-identification-number-itin) provides the first two procedures. The [Instructions for Form W-7](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iw7) govern the later application package.

## How do you request a federal extension without an ITIN?

Complete and mail Form 4868 by the due date that applies to your return. In each field requesting the missing SSN or ITIN, write **ITIN TO BE REQUESTED**. Do not include Form W-7 with the extension request. The IRS gives these exact steps under [“Apply for an extension without an ITIN”](https://www.irs.gov/tin/itin/individual-taxpayer-identification-number-itin).

Form 4868 covers several individual return types, including Form 1040-NR. The applicable original deadline can differ by return and filer, so use the current form instructions rather than assuming every nonresident has the same April deadline.

The extension addresses filing time only. The [Form 4868 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-access/f4868_accessible.pdf) require an estimate of total tax liability and warn that unpaid tax can accrue interest and may incur penalties. Obtain qualified tax advice if you cannot determine whether a return or payment is required.

## How do you make an estimated payment without an ITIN?

The IRS says income without sufficient withholding, including self-employment income, may require estimated payments. A missing ITIN does not remove that possibility.

For the no-ITIN procedure described by the IRS:

1. Use the current Form 1040-ES or Form 1040-ES (NR) estimated-tax payment voucher.
2. Write **ITIN TO BE REQUESTED** in the voucher field for an SSN or ITIN.
3. Mail the voucher and check or money order to the address listed in the current form.
4. Keep a copy of the voucher and proof of payment with the later W-7 and return records.
5. Do not attach Form W-7 to Form 1040-ES.

These steps are the paper procedure on the [IRS ITIN page](https://www.irs.gov/tin/itin/individual-taxpayer-identification-number-itin). They do not establish the amount due or guarantee matching without follow-up. Use the current form-year instructions.

## When should Form W-7 be submitted?

Form W-7 normally belongs with the return for which the ITIN is needed. A first-time applicant attaches W-7 to the front of the original return and leaves the applicant's SSN area blank. A qualifying exception applicant follows that exception's evidence rules instead.

If the package cannot be completed by the original due date, request an extension by that date. When ready, send it through an approved ITIN route—not the ordinary Form 1040-NR address.

An extension does not create a federal tax purpose for an ITIN. You still must be ineligible for an SSN and have a supported W-7 reason. Owning a U.S. LLC or needing the LLC's EIN is not, by itself, a personal ITIN reason.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I e-file Form 4868 without an ITIN?

The IRS's specific no-ITIN procedure says to complete and mail Form 4868 with “ITIN TO BE REQUESTED” in the SSN or ITIN fields. Electronic providers may require a numeric TIN, so do not assume an e-file workflow will accept the phrase. Follow the current IRS paper instructions when no number exists.

### Should I attach Form W-7 to my extension or estimated payment?

No. The IRS explicitly says not to include Form W-7 with Form 4868 or Form 1040-ES. Submit W-7 later with the qualifying federal return, unless you meet a listed exception to the return-attachment rule.

### Does Form 4868 extend the deadline to pay tax?

No. It extends the time to file the return, not the time to pay. Estimate and pay any amount due by the applicable payment deadline to limit possible interest and penalties.
