**Form5472 Prep is a preparation-and-fax service for foreign-owned US single-member LLCs. Our team prepares Form 5472 and pro forma Form 1120, a tax accountant who holds an IRS PTIN reviews, you check and sign online, and we fax to the IRS Ogden PIN Unit with a receipt. We are not a CPA firm.**

This is our explanation of the service and its limits. The [About page](/about), [security policy](/security), [privacy policy](/privacy) and [terms](/terms) set out the process, handling of your information and responsibilities on both sides.

## What does Form5472 Prep do?

We prepare and fax the Form 5472 and pro forma Form 1120 package for foreign-owned US single-member LLCs treated as disregarded entities. You complete a guided intake form about the LLC, its owner and the year's transactions. Our team prepares the package from those answers, then sends it through review before you sign.

The package includes a cover letter and Part V supporting statement, plus a reasonable cause statement for a late filing. We handle current and past tax years, including dormant-LLC and final-year filings. See the [Form 5472 filing service](/services/form-5472-filing-service) for that filing scope.

After you check and sign online, we fax the signed package to the IRS Ogden PIN Unit and send the timestamped fax receipt. The receipt records transmission; it is not an IRS acceptance notice.

## Who reviews the filing?

Every filing is reviewed by a tax accountant who holds an IRS PTIN before submission. The review checks the prepared package against your answers. If something needs clarification, we message you on your filing page. Signing opens after review approval, and you can ask for a change before signing.

You or another person authorized for the LLC signs the return. Our staff never sign it for you, and nothing is sent to the IRS until you have signed. You remain responsible for the accuracy and completeness of the information you supply.

## What is it not?

We are not a CPA firm. We do not give tax advice, tax planning or legal advice, and we do not provide IRS representation. Our work is preparing and submitting forms from your information. We are not affiliated with the IRS or any government agency, and we cannot promise IRS acceptance or penalty relief.

We do not prepare Form 1065, a full Form 1120 for an LLC taxed as a corporation, Form 1040-NR or state returns. We do not keep your books. Form 7004 extension filing is a separate paid service, rather than part of the Form 5472 filing fee.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does payment mean the IRS has accepted my filing?

No. Payment covers the preparation-and-fax service. You check and sign after review, then we transmit the package. The fax receipt shows transmission, not IRS acceptance.

### Can I use Express for the same filing as Standard?

Yes. Both tiers include the same package, accountant review and IRS fax delivery. Express changes the turnaround to ready to sign in under 3 days, 7 days a week; you still need to check and sign.

### Will you represent me if the IRS questions the return?

No. We do not provide IRS representation or tax advice. Our service prepares and submits the filing package; it does not promise how the IRS will process it or decide any penalty.
