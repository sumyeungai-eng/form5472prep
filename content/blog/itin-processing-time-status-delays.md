## The ITIN timeline has three separate stages

Treating every day as “IRS processing” makes the wait hard to diagnose. Separate the timeline:

| Stage | Who controls it | What can extend it |
|---|---|---|
| Application preparation | Applicant and preparer | Missing federal purpose, return, exception evidence, or identity documents |
| Delivery to the ITIN Unit | Sender and carrier | International transit, address errors, or no tracking |
| IRS processing | IRS | Peak season, overseas volume, document review, errors, or additional-information requests |

The published seven- or nine-to-eleven-week window is the third stage. Add preparation and shipping time when planning around a tax return or transaction.

## What does the IRS processing-status page mean?

The [IRS processing-status page](https://www.irs.gov/help/processing-status-for-tax-forms) reports the receipt month currently being worked for Form W-7. On September 3, 2026, it displayed **July 2026**. That is a dated operational snapshot, not an approval forecast for a particular applicant.

Your file may move faster or slower because the page does not show whether it requires error correction, exception review, document validation, or correspondence. Check the page again before publication or before making a time-sensitive decision; the receipt month changes as the queue advances.

## When should you check your ITIN status?

The [IRS ITIN page](https://www.irs.gov/tin/itin/individual-taxpayer-identification-number-itin) says to contact the IRS if no notice arrives within the applicable timeframe. Before calling, have:

- the applicant’s full legal name;
- mailing address used on Form W-7;
- date the package was delivered;
- carrier tracking or other proof of receipt;
- a copy of the signed Form W-7;
- the attached return or exception category; and
- any IRS notice already received.

Do not send a duplicate W-7 simply because the application is still inside the normal window. A second package can create confusion rather than speed.

## Five common causes of avoidable delay

1. **No valid federal tax purpose.** An ITIN is not issued solely for banking, ecommerce, or general identification.
2. **Missing tax return or exception evidence.** Most new applications include a U.S. federal return unless a specific exception applies.
3. **Inconsistent personal information.** Names, dates, citizenship, passport information, and addresses must align across the W-7 and evidence.
4. **Unacceptable or expired documents.** The IRS applies document-specific currency, photo, and dependent-residency rules.
5. **Unreachable mailing address.** The IRS sends the assignment or rejection notice to the address on the application.

The current [Form W-7 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iw7) are the source of truth for the package. A CAA can help identify completeness problems before submission, but no preparer can control the IRS queue.
