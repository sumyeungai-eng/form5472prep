## Is an EIN service legit?

An EIN service can legitimately prepare Form SS-4 and handle authorised IRS contact. Check the contact, form review, deliverables and data policies. A service reduces administrative work and preventable error risk; the IRS controls the queue.

A PTIN is a registration, not an endorsement. The IRS [choosing a tax professional guidance](https://www.irs.gov/tax-professionals/choosing-a-tax-professional) says anyone with a PTIN can be a paid tax return preparer. The IRS directory lists CPAs, enrolled agents, attorneys and annual-filing-season participants. An EIN application is not a tax return, and neither Form SS-4 nor its instructions mention a PTIN or any preparer credential.

## What information does an EIN application actually require?

The [IRS Instructions for Form SS-4, revised December 2025](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iss4), require applicable entity, address, ownership, classification and business information, plus a signature. Form SS-4 has no line for a passport or date of birth.

Formation records establish the legal name, state and formation date. Ask providers to distinguish SS-4 information from documents requested for the provider's own purposes.

## Does an EIN service need my passport?

Neither Form SS-4 nor its instructions call for a passport or any identity-document copy. The instructions are silent on passports and attaching identity documents. Line 7b takes the responsible party's SSN or ITIN, or permits “foreign” when the responsible party has neither and is ineligible to obtain one.

An ITIN is a separate personal tax number, with a different application. Form5472 Prep's EIN intake has no passport upload, no ID-copy requirement and nothing to certify. For any document Form SS-4 does not require, ask the provider for the reason in writing.

## Can a provider make the IRS issue an EIN faster?

No provider controls IRS issuance. The IRS Instructions for Form SS-4 (revised December 2025) state that applicants who fax Form SS-4 under the Fax-TIN program can receive their EIN by fax generally within 4 business days, and that applicants who mail the form receive their EIN in approximately 4 weeks.

The IRS Instructions for Form SS-4 (revised December 2025) estimate the average time across all respondents at 5 hours 58 minutes for recordkeeping, 1 hour 17 minutes learning about the law or the form, and 1 hour 26 minutes preparing, copying, assembling and sending: 8 hours 41 minutes total.

The burden estimate describes applicant work, not IRS processing. The instructions give no telephone-processing estimate. Keep provider delivery estimates separate from IRS timing.

## Can someone else apply for an EIN for me?

Yes. A third-party designee with valid signed authority can answer SS-4 questions and receive the newly assigned EIN. A third-party designee's authority ends when the EIN is assigned and released to the designee. The IRS mails the EIN notice to the taxpayer.

A nominee cannot apply for an EIN. The IRS [responsible parties and nominees guidance](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/responsible-parties-and-nominees) distinguishes nominees from the true owner or controller. Read the [designee guide](/blog/ein-third-party-designee-apply-on-your-behalf) for the authorisation procedure.

## How do you verify any EIN provider before you send documents in 2026?

1. **Confirm the service fee.** Get written deliverables and total price. The IRS fee is $0.
2. **Check the responsible party.** Ask whose name appears on lines 7a-7b. The responsible party must be the natural person who ultimately owns or controls the LLC, never a substitute provider.
3. **Request the completed SS-4.** Review before submission, never sign a blank form, and keep the signed final copy.
4. **Challenge extra requests.** Compare each request with the table. Obtain a written reason for anything Form SS-4 does not require.
5. **Read the data policies.** Look for named retention periods, service providers, a deletion route and a breach-notice window. Ask about missing specifics before sending documents.
6. **Ask about timing limits.** Have the provider explain what the provider will not promise. IRS issuance remains outside the provider's control.
7. **Check the assignment evidence.** Confirm the IRS notice arrives by mail to the LLC as taxpayer. If in doubt, use 800-829-4933, the verification number in the SS-4 instructions, to verify an EIN.
