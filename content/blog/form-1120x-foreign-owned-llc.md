## What does Form 1120-X actually amend?

Form 1120-X amends a corporation's income tax return. The [Instructions for Form 1120-X, Rev. 12/2025](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1120x), describe correcting Form 1120 as originally filed or subsequently adjusted. Certain late elections are another purpose. The Form 1120 instructions also direct correction of a previously filed Form 1120 through Form 1120-X.

Form 1120-X uses columns for original or adjusted amounts, net changes and corrected amounts. Part II explains changes. The remaining mechanics address recalculating tax, payments, overpayments and refunds. The corporate amendment form therefore fits an income-reporting return directly.

## Does Form 1120-X cover a pro forma Form 1120?

A foreign-owned disregarded entity files a pro forma Form 1120. The [Form 5472 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i5472) require Form 5472 as an attachment despite the DE having no income tax return filing requirement. The Form 1120 instructions explain that corporate treatment applies for limited section 6038A purposes.

The required cover entries are the DE's name, address, and items B and E on page one. Item B is the EIN. The cover also carries “Foreign-owned U.S. DE” across the top. The [pro forma Form 1120 guide](/blog/pro-forma-form-1120-foreign-owned-llc) explains the limited cover function.

General Form 1120 amendment wording is broad. However, the reviewed Form 1120-X instructions never mention DEs, pro forma returns or Form 5472. The verified conclusion remains narrow: the instructions neither expressly require nor expressly prohibit Form 1120-X for a DE's pro forma cover. Mandatory use, prohibition and guaranteed acceptance would all exceed the sources.

## Can you amend Form 5472?

Form 5472 reports related-party transactions. A corrected information package is a practical preparer recommendation, but the Form 5472 instructions (Rev. December 2024) contain no amended-return procedure. Form 5472 (Rev. December 2023) has no amended-return checkbox. Form 1120 item E lists Initial return, Final return, Name change and Address change, with no amended box. Correcting Form 5472 requires correcting the information return itself. For package assembly, use the [guide to correcting a filed Form 5472](/blog/amended-form-5472-correcting-errors).

## What if the LLC filed a full Form 1120 by mistake?

A mistakenly filed income-reporting Form 1120 requires separate review. General Form 1120-X wording suggests a possible correction instrument, but the instructions give no DE-specific procedure for reversing the mistake. Treating Form 1120-X as the remedy is an inference requiring professional judgment.

Have a qualified tax adviser review classification, the submitted income return and any tax paid. Do not assume a replacement pro forma cover resolves the corporate return already submitted. Correcting DE information and resolving the income return are distinct tasks.

## How do you file a corrected Form 5472 and pro forma 1120?

A preparer should select the correction instrument before submission. The following workflow is preparer judgment where special DE instructions are silent; the [correcting-errors guide](/blog/amended-form-5472-correcting-errors) covers detailed assembly.

1. **Choose the instrument.** Confirm tax classification and whether the original Form 1120 reported income or served as a pro forma cover. Consider Form 1120-X separately when an income return needs correction.
2. **Label the correction.** Identify the LLC, EIN, tax year and original filing in a correction explanation. Clear correction labelling is preparer practice, not an IRS checkbox procedure.
3. **Arrange signing.** Identify the authorized signer. Signing and dating the pro forma cover is a conservative preparer workflow; special DE instructions do not expressly resolve the signature question. Form 1120-X has its own signing instructions.
4. **Select the route.** Apply the dedicated Ogden route to the corrected DE package as preparer judgment. Follow Form 1120-X's service-center instruction for a corporate amendment.
5. **Retain delivery evidence.** Keep the exact submitted package and transmission or mailing evidence together. A fax receipt documents transmission, not IRS processing, acceptance or penalty relief.

## Where do you send a corrected Form 5472?

The IRS Ogden PIN Unit receives a foreign-owned DE's Form 5472 package. The Form 5472 instructions specify fax at 300 DPI or higher to **855-887-7737, the IRS Ogden fax line**, or mail to:

Internal Revenue Service  
1973 Rulon White Blvd  
M/S 6112, Attn: PIN Unit  
Ogden, UT 84201

Applying the original DE route to a corrected DE package is preparer judgment; the instructions state no separate correction destination. Foreign-owned U.S. DEs cannot e-file Form 5472. Online fax delivery remains fax transmission.

Form 1120-X goes to the applicable service center where the corporation filed the original return. The service-center wording does not independently authorize faxing Form 1120-X to Ogden. The instructions do not resolve Form 1120-X routing for a pro forma DE correction.

## Does the Form 1120-X deadline limit a Form 5472 correction?

The IRS generally requires a corporation to file Form 1120-X within three years after filing its original return or two years after paying tax if claiming a refund, whichever is later (Instructions for Form 1120-X, Rev. 12/2025). An early-filed return is treated as filed on the due date; special rules exist.

The Form 5472 instructions state no corresponding correction cutoff. Corporate amendment and refund wording should not become an invented deadline for correcting DE information.

[IRC 6501(c)(8)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26/6501) addresses tax assessment separately. For missing section 6038A information, assessment for a related return, event or period cannot expire before three years after the required information is furnished. If reasonable cause and absence of willful neglect are established, the extension applies only to related items.

Section 6501(c)(8) does not itself establish a Form 5472 penalty deadline or create tax where none is owed. The [Form 5472 statute-of-limitations guide](/blog/form-5472-statute-of-limitations) explains the distinction.

## Does correcting remove the $25,000 penalty exposure?

A correction does not guarantee penalty cancellation. The IRS assesses $25,000 on a reporting corporation that fails to file Form 5472 when due and in the prescribed manner (Instructions for Form 5472, Rev. December 2024). The instructions treat a substantially incomplete return as unfiled and also cover required-records failures.

Continued failure beyond 90 days after IRS notification carries an additional $25,000 per related party for each subsequent 30-day period or fraction (Form 5472 instructions). Correction does not itself start or reset the notice-based clock.

[Regulation 1.6038A-4(d)(3)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/part-1/section-1.6038A-4) provides for cessation of accrual, including furnishing information to substantially complete Form 5472. The paragraph specifies compliance for the examination year and subsequent years to the District Director's satisfaction; sending corrected paper alone is no guarantee.

Paragraph (b)(1) addresses reasonable cause and the beginning of the notice-based period. Paragraph (b)(2)(i) requires an affirmative factual showing in a written statement under penalties of perjury. Paragraph (b)(2)(i) also provides that an omission or error does not constitute a failure if the corporation establishes substantial compliance to the IRS's satisfaction. None of the provisions promises automatic cancellation upon correction. For an existing notice, consult the [penalty-notice guide](/blog/form-5472-penalty-notice-what-to-do).

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Form 1120X required for a foreign-owned disregarded LLC?

The reviewed instructions do not expressly require Form 1120-X for a DE's pro forma cover. General Form 1120 amendment wording points to Form 1120-X, but the special DE instructions leave the overlap unresolved. A corrected DE package is a preparer recommendation.

### Is Form 1120-X prohibited for a pro forma Form 1120?

No prohibition appears in the reviewed instructions. Silence also does not establish mandatory use or guaranteed acceptance. A preparer considering Form 1120-X for a pro forma cover needs a reason grounded in the actual filing.

### Can Form 1120-X replace a corrected Form 5472?

No. Form 1120-X addresses the corporate return and tax-return amounts. Correcting related-party or transaction information requires correcting Form 5472 itself. A Form 1120-X explanation alone is not a replacement information return.

### Is there an amended checkbox on Form 1120 item E?

No. Item E lists Initial return, Final return, Name change and Address change. Clear correction labelling on a pro forma package is preparer practice, not an IRS amended-return checkbox procedure.

### Does an LLC with a corporate election use Form 1120-X?

An LLC that elected association treatment through Form 8832 and properly files a corporate Form 1120 uses ordinary Form 1120-X correction rules. The unresolved question concerns the DE's pro forma cover.

### Do I have only three years to correct Form 5472?

The reviewed Form 5472 instructions state no correction cutoff. Form 1120-X's general three-year/two-year wording should not be imported as an information-return deadline. IRC 6501(c)(8) concerns assessment of related tax, subject to a reasonable-cause limitation.

### Will submitting a corrected package cancel a penalty?

No automatic cancellation is stated. Regulation 1.6038A-4 addresses cessation of accrual, reasonable cause and substantial compliance. Correcting forms does not guarantee relief from the original failure. An existing notice needs separate review.
