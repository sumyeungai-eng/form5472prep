**Fax a foreign-owned US disregarded entity's complete Form 5472 package at 300 DPI or higher to the IRS Ogden PIN Unit at 855-887-7737. Include the pro forma Form 1120, every required Form 5472 and attachment, and any signed statement. Keep the full transmitted file and a timestamped successful-transmission receipt as filing evidence.**

The fax is the submission, not a draft handoff. If the package is not complete, [use the guided preparation process](/start?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=internal&utm_campaign=how-to-fax-form-5472) before sending it.

## What documents belong in the fax?

The minimum package for a foreign-owned US disregarded entity generally contains:

1. A pro forma Form 1120 with “Foreign-owned U.S. DE” written across the top.
2. A completed Form 5472 for each related party with reportable transactions.
3. A Part V attachment describing formation, contributions, distributions and other reportable transactions.
4. Any ownership-attribution, exchange-rate or other attachment required by the facts.
5. A signed reasonable cause statement for a late return when one is being asserted.

Do not fax [Form 5472](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f5472.pdf) alone when a pro forma Form 1120 is required. The [IRS instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i5472) state that Form 5472 is attached to the pro forma return.

## What is the correct Form 5472 fax number?

The current IRS fax number for a foreign-owned US disregarded entity is **855-887-7737**. The instructions require a resolution of **300 DPI or higher**.

Before every filing, verify the number on the current IRS instructions rather than relying on an old bookmark or formation-company article. Tax forms and routing instructions can change.

## How do you send Form 5472 correctly?

1. **Create one final PDF.** Put the pro forma 1120 first, followed by Form 5472 and clearly labelled attachments.
2. **Run a page preflight.** Confirm entity name, EIN, tax year, signatures and page order.
3. **Set resolution.** Use 300 DPI or higher and check that small numbers remain readable.
4. **Enter 855-887-7737.** Recheck every digit before sending.
5. **Transmit once and monitor.** Wait for a final successful status rather than assuming “queued” means delivered.
6. **Save evidence.** Store the exact PDF, fax receipt, timestamp, destination number and page count together.
7. **Review failures promptly.** Correct busy, partial or failed transmissions before the filing deadline.

This seven-step protocol is our filing-evidence standard because a readable return without delivery proof and a receipt without the matching PDF are each incomplete records.

## Should the fax have a cover sheet?

A concise cover sheet can identify the entity, EIN, tax year, form and page count. Do not put unnecessary sensitive owner data on it. The cover sheet does not replace the required forms or signatures.

Confirm that the receipt's transmitted page count matches the intended package. A mismatch can indicate a partial transmission.

## What proof does the IRS send back?

The IRS does not routinely send an acceptance acknowledgement for this faxed package. The sender's transmission record is therefore important evidence. Keep:

- the exact final PDF;
- the successful status and timestamp;
- the destination number;
- the page count;
- the fax provider's message or job ID; and
- any later IRS correspondence.

## Can Form 5472 be mailed instead?

Yes. The IRS instructions list this dedicated address:

> Internal Revenue Service  
> 1973 Rulon White Blvd  
> M/S 6112 Attn: PIN Unit  
> Ogden, UT 84201

Use trackable delivery and keep a full copy. Do not use the ordinary Form 1120 service-centre address for a foreign-owned US disregarded entity.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a foreign-owned LLC e-file Form 5472?

No. The current instructions say a foreign-owned US disregarded entity cannot electronically file Form 5472; it must fax or mail the package to the dedicated Ogden PIN Unit.

### What resolution should the fax use?

Use 300 DPI or higher. Review the rendered pages before sending so identifiers, amounts and attachment text are legible.

### Is a queued fax proof of filing?

No. Keep a final successful-transmission status with timestamp, destination number and page count. A queued or processing status does not prove delivery.

### Should Form 7004 use the same fax number?

Yes. The Form 5472 instructions direct a foreign-owned US disregarded entity to fax or mail Form 7004 to the same dedicated destination by the regular due date.

### What if the fax failed before the deadline?

Correct the transmission and resend promptly. Preserve both the failed attempt and the successful receipt, especially when timing may later matter.
