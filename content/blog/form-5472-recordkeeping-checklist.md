**Last updated: September 2026**

A foreign-owned U.S. LLC should keep records that prove the identity, date, amount, currency, business purpose, and classification of every related-party transaction reported on Form 5472. Bank statements alone are not enough when transfers have vague descriptions. Retain invoices, owner ledgers, loan documents, reimbursement support, exchange-rate evidence, and copies of filed forms and delivery receipts.

Once the year-end packet is complete, [start the Form 5472 filing](/start).

## What does the IRS require a reporting corporation to keep?

The [IRS Instructions for Form 5472](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i5472) require permanent books of account or records sufficient to establish the correctness of the federal return and the treatment of related-party transactions. The instructions also state that failure to maintain required records can trigger the $25,000 Form 5472 penalty.

The detailed [Treasury record-maintenance rule](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-26/section-1.6038A-3) covers records relevant to transactions between the reporting corporation and related parties.

## What belongs in the year-end Form 5472 packet?

Use five folders:

1. **Entity:** EIN notice, articles of organization, operating agreement, formation date, addresses, and ownership record.
2. **Owner:** legal name, address, country of citizenship and tax residence, FTIN, and consistent reference ID.
3. **Transactions:** LLC bank statements, owner-account statements showing transfers, and payment-platform exports.
4. **Support:** invoices, receipts, reimbursement reports, loan notes, interest schedules, and noncash valuation support.
5. **Filing:** final signed Form 5472, pro forma Form 1120, Part V statement, extension if any, and fax or mailing evidence.

The Form5472 Prep evidence rule is simple: **every reported total should trace to a schedule, and every schedule line should trace to source evidence**.

## How should owner transfers be documented?

Create a transaction schedule with these columns:

| Field | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Date | Fixes the tax year and exchange-rate date |
| Sender and recipient | Establishes related-party identity |
| Source amount/currency | Preserves the original transaction |
| USD amount/rate | Supports the number on the form |
| Category | Contribution, distribution, loan, repayment, interest, service, or reimbursement |
| Purpose | Explains why the transfer occurred |
| Source document | Links the total to proof |

Do not use “transfer” as the final category. “Owner contribution for operating cash” and “repayment of owner loan principal” tell the reviewer what actually happened.

## How long should Form 5472 records be kept?

The Form 5472 instructions say books and records should be retained for as long as their contents may become material in administering federal tax law. That standard is fact-dependent and can be longer than a generic three-year rule.

Keep filed returns and proof of delivery permanently. Preserve the supporting records for at least as long as they can affect an open tax year, a related-party balance, a basis calculation, a loan, or a later IRS inquiry. Ask a qualified tax professional for a retention policy tailored to the LLC.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I attach bank statements to Form 5472?

Generally no. Keep statements as support. The filed package reports totals and includes the required Part V description rather than every source document.

### Are screenshots enough?

Screenshots can supplement records, but downloadable statements, invoices, and transaction exports are stronger because they preserve dates, parties, and complete details.

### Do I keep records for unrelated customer sales?

Keep ordinary accounting records for all business activity. The Form 5472 workpaper should separately identify the transactions involving related parties.

### Is a fax receipt proof that the form was correct?

No. A fax receipt proves delivery. The signed forms, supporting schedule, and source records prove what was filed and how the totals were calculated.
