**Last updated: July 2026**

A foreign-owned U.S. disregarded entity should enter the owner’s foreign taxpayer identification number if one exists. If the owner has no U.S. identifying number, a consistent filer-created reference ID may be required. Enter the owner’s complete residential or business address using the country’s normal components; do not substitute the LLC’s registered-agent address for the owner’s address.

## What is an FTIN on Form 5472?

An FTIN is the taxpayer identification number issued by the owner’s country of tax administration. Examples include national tax numbers used on the owner’s local tax return. It is not the U.S. LLC’s EIN.

The [IRS Instructions for Form 5472](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i5472) state that a foreign-owned U.S. disregarded entity must enter an FTIN, if any, for each listed foreign owner. If the owner has no FTIN, the instructions permit “None” or “N/A.”

## What is a Form 5472 reference ID?

A reference ID is an identifier created by or for the reporting LLC when a foreign owner or related party lacks a U.S. identifying number. The IRS does not issue it.

| Rule | Practical action |
|---|---|
| Alphanumeric only | Use letters and numbers |
| No spaces or special characters | Avoid hyphens, slashes, and punctuation |
| Maximum 50 characters | Choose a short durable format |
| Consistent every year | Store it in the permanent owner record |
| Never recycle it | Do not assign an old ID to a new person |

A durable example format is `OWNER1COUNTRY2026`. The exact characters are less important than consistency and uniqueness. Never embed a passport number or other sensitive identifier when a neutral internal code will work.

## Which address belongs in each part of the form?

The LLC’s address identifies the reporting corporation. The owner’s address identifies the foreign shareholder or related party. Those addresses can differ.

Use the LLC’s actual mailing address in Part I based on the entity’s records. Use the owner’s complete foreign address in Part II and, when the owner is also the related party, Part III. Include street, city, province or state, postal code, and country where applicable.

The [current Form 5472](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f5472.pdf) provides separate fields for the reporting corporation and the foreign owner. A registered-agent address should not automatically be copied into the owner field.

## What is the Form5472 Prep identity audit?

Before submission, compare:

1. LLC legal name and EIN against the IRS EIN notice.
2. LLC address against the current entity record.
3. Owner legal name against foreign tax or identity records.
4. FTIN against the owner’s local tax document.
5. Reference ID against the prior-year Form 5472.
6. Country fields against citizenship, organization, tax residence, and principal business activity.

The country questions are not interchangeable. “Country of citizenship,” “country of tax residence,” and “principal country where business is conducted” can have different answers.

## Frequently asked questions

### What if my country does not issue an FTIN?

The IRS instructions allow “None” or “N/A” for a foreign-owned U.S. disregarded entity when the owner has no FTIN.

### Is the reference ID the LLC’s EIN?

No. The EIN identifies the U.S. LLC. A reference ID identifies a foreign owner or related party that lacks a U.S. identifying number.

### Can I change the reference ID next year?

Use the same reference ID every year for the same person. If a change is unavoidable, follow the IRS correlation rules for old and new IDs.

### Can I use the registered-agent address for the owner?

Not merely for convenience. Enter the address that actually identifies the foreign owner; the LLC’s address belongs in the reporting-corporation section.
