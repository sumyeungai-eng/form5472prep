## A specialist back office without surrendering the relationship

White label is not simply placing a logo on a document. It is an operating agreement about who the client sees, who gathers the information, who prepares the work, who reviews it, who answers questions, and who preserves the filing record.

For the standard foreign-owned US disregarded LLC profile, Form5472 Prep prepares:

- Form 5472;
- the limited pro forma Form 1120;
- the supporting Part V statement where applicable;
- a reasonable-cause statement for supported late-filing cases;
- the signature workflow; and
- IRS fax delivery with a timestamped submission receipt.

Every package receives review by a qualified tax accountant before submission. The [About page](/about) explains the filing-courier model and its boundaries.

## What to define in a white-label agreement

Before sending the first client, agree on five practical items.

### Brand touchpoints

List the communications that should use the firm's brand. Do not assume that requesting white label automatically changes every portal page, URL, invoice, email sender, or government form. Government filings must still show the legally required taxpayer and preparer information.

### Client communication

Decide whether client questions go first to the accounting firm or directly to Form5472 Prep. A single visible owner prevents duplicate or conflicting answers.

### Scope escalation

Form 5472 is an information return. A client may also need income-tax, withholding, state, treaty, or entity-classification advice. Define who handles those questions before a complex file arrives.

### Document custody

Agree who retains formation documents, owner ledgers, signed packages, and fax receipts. The partner dashboard provides filing visibility, but the accounting firm should still maintain its engagement records under its own policies.

### Billing

The standard partner program uses per-filing checkout at the same current prices as direct customers. Volume pricing or consolidated invoicing can be discussed. Any client-facing fee or markup should be agreed between the accounting firm and its client and should not be confused with an unconfirmed commission from Form5472 Prep.

## When white label is a strong fit

White-label delivery is particularly useful when:

- the firm wants one consistent brand across bookkeeping, formation, and annual compliance;
- foreign-owned LLC work is recurring but not frequent enough for a dedicated internal specialist;
- the firm wants to retain its client relationship rather than make an external referral;
- staff need a repeatable intake and status workflow; or
- the firm serves clients across time zones and needs a browser-based signature and documented submission trail.

The standard partner program may be simpler when speed matters more than brand continuity or when the accounting firm is comfortable telling the client that a specialist filing provider is involved.

## Questions to ask before outsourcing a Form 5472 workflow

Evaluate any provider against operational facts rather than broad “full-service tax” language:

- Does the service clearly distinguish Form 5472 information reporting from owner income-tax advice?
- Does it prepare the pro forma Form 1120 as part of the package?
- Who reviews the package before submission?
- Who obtains the taxpayer's signature?
- Which submission route is used, and what proof is retained?
- Can the firm monitor every client file without sharing client passwords?
- What happens when a file is outside the standard profile?
- Which communications are actually white-labeled?

Those answers belong in the operating agreement before client data begins moving.
