**A foreign-owned Florida single-member LLC generally files a Sunbiz annual report each year to remain active and may also need federal Form 5472 attached to a pro forma Form 1120 when it has a reportable transaction with its owner. The annual report is due by May 1; a calendar-year federal package is generally due April 15.**

These are separate filings. Sunbiz maintains the Florida entity record; Form 5472 reports related-party transactions to the IRS. If the owner funded or withdrew money from the company, [start the federal filing here](/start?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=internal&utm_campaign=florida-foreign-owner).

## What are the annual requirements for a Florida LLC?

Use this two-agency checklist:

| Filing | Agency | 2026 rule |
|---|---|---|
| Florida annual report | Florida Division of Corporations | File online between January 1 and May 1 |
| Florida LLC annual-report fee | Florida Division of Corporations | $138.75 before the deadline |
| Federal Form 5472 + pro forma 1120 | IRS | Generally April 15 for a calendar-year foreign-owned DE with a reportable transaction |
| Form 7004 extension | IRS Ogden PIN Unit | File by the regular federal due date for six extra months |

The [official Sunbiz filing page](https://services.sunbiz.org/Filings/AnnualReport/FilingStart?Length=12) says annual reports are due between January 1 and May 1. Florida's [LLC filing booklet](https://form.sunbiz.org/pdf/605_booklet.pdf) lists a $138.75 LLC annual-report fee and a $400 late fee after May 1.

## What does the Florida annual report do?

The annual report updates or confirms the LLC's public record, including its principal address, registered agent and authorised persons. It is not a financial report and does not calculate federal tax.

The first report is due in the calendar year after formation. Filing late adds the statutory fee; failing to restore compliance can eventually affect active status. Use Sunbiz directly rather than a lookalike solicitation.

## When does Form 5472 apply to a Florida LLC?

Under the [IRS Form 5472 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i5472), a US disregarded entity wholly owned by a foreign person generally files when it has a reportable transaction with the owner or another related party.

Common Florida-owner triggers include:

- the owner paying state formation or annual-report costs personally;
- capital transferred into the LLC;
- owner loans or repayments;
- withdrawals and distributions;
- personal expenses paid by the LLC; and
- formation, acquisition, sale or dissolution events.

Sunbiz status does not answer the federal question. An active LLC can have no Form 5472 obligation in a genuinely transaction-free year, while a dormant LLC can have an obligation because the owner paid its annual fee.

## Does Florida charge an LLC franchise tax?

Florida does not impose a Delaware-style flat LLC franchise tax merely for keeping a default disregarded LLC active. The annual-report fee is an entity-maintenance charge. Separate Florida income, reemployment, sales or other taxes can apply based on elections, employees and business activity.

Do not use that general point to conclude that nothing else is due. A corporate tax election or Florida-source activity changes the analysis.

## What is the best 2026 filing calendar?

1. **January:** reconcile owner activity and verify the Sunbiz record.
2. **By April 15:** file Form 5472 with its pro forma Form 1120, or timely request the federal extension.
3. **By May 1:** file the Florida annual report and pay the fee.
4. **After filing:** save both confirmations in separate state and federal folders.
5. **Before closing:** file the proper Florida dissolution and prepare any final federal return.

The one-month spacing makes it practical to complete the federal ledger first and then confirm state status.

## How does a Florida foreign owner file Form 5472?

1. Confirm single-member foreign ownership and federal classification.
2. Reconcile all owner and related-party transfers.
3. Prepare Form 5472 and its Part V statement.
4. Attach it to a pro forma Form 1120 marked “Foreign-owned U.S. DE.”
5. Fax to 855-887-7737 at 300 DPI or higher, or mail to the dedicated Ogden PIN Unit address.
6. Keep the exact filed package and timestamped receipt.

## Frequently asked questions

### When is the 2026 Florida LLC annual report due?

It is due by May 1, 2026. Sunbiz accepts reports from January 1, and the state adds a $400 late fee after May 1.

### Does the annual report replace Form 5472?

No. The Florida annual report maintains the public entity record. Form 5472 reports transactions between a foreign-owned LLC and related parties to the IRS.

### Does a no-income Florida LLC file Form 5472?

It may. Owner funding, personally paid annual-report fees, withdrawals or reimbursements can be reportable even with no customer revenue.

### Does Florida require an annual-report balance sheet?

No. The Sunbiz annual report updates entity and management details; it is not a financial statement or income-tax return.

### What if the LLC was formed in 2026?

Its first Florida annual report is generally due in 2027, but its first federal Form 5472 may be due for 2026 if formation or funding transactions occurred.
