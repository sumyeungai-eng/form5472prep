Yes—an eligible applicant can apply for an ITIN without a valid passport, but must use an acceptable combination of supporting documents. A passport is the only standalone identity and foreign-status document; without it, the IRS generally requires at least two current documents that together establish both, with a photograph subject to limited dependent exceptions. An expired passport does not become acceptable merely because a copy is certified. [Sources: IRS supporting-document requirements](https://www.irs.gov/tin/itin/itin-supporting-documents), [Tax Topic 857](https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc857).

This guide focuses on adult nonresident applicants. Children and applicants claimed as dependents can have additional age, relationship, and US-residency requirements.

## First confirm that you need an ITIN

Documents establish who you are; they do not establish that you qualify for an ITIN. You need a federal tax purpose and must not be eligible for an SSN. Form W-7 generally goes with a federal tax return unless a listed exception applies and you provide the required exception evidence. Owning an LLC or wanting a US bank account does not, by itself, establish a qualifying application. [Source: Form W-7 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iw7).

If the number you actually need is the company's tax identifier, review the [EIN service and eligibility information](/ein) before preparing a personal application. An EIN belongs to the entity; an ITIN is an individual's federal tax-processing number.

## Compare the document routes

The following table is a pre-application checklist based on the IRS document rules. It is not a guarantee that a particular document will be accepted.

| Document or proposed combination | What it can establish | Important limit |
|---|---|---|
| Valid passport | Identity and foreign status | Certain dependents need additional US-residency evidence |
| Qualifying national identification card plus another accepted document | A national ID can support identity and foreign status | It is not standalone; check required name, photo, address, birthdate, and expiration information |
| Current foreign driver's license plus foreign civil birth certificate | License supports identity; foreign birth certificate supports foreign status | Both documents must meet IRS requirements and identify the same applicant |
| Two driver's licenses | Identity evidence | Does not supply foreign-status evidence merely because one license is foreign |
| Expired passport plus an ordinary photocopy of another ID | An incomplete proposed package | Expiration and copy requirements remain unresolved |

[Source: IRS acceptable supporting documents and additional requirements](https://www.irs.gov/tin/itin/itin-supporting-documents). The IRS accepts a defined list of 13 document types. Do not substitute bank statements, utility bills, or an LLC formation certificate for adult identity and foreign-status evidence just because they display your name.

## Current documents and certified copies are separate tests

The IRS requires supporting identification to be current on the date the application is submitted. A civil birth certificate is treated as current because it has no expiration date. Other documents must satisfy their applicable validity requirements. [Source: IRS supporting-document requirements](https://www.irs.gov/tin/itin/itin-supporting-documents).

The copy rule is different: the IRS generally requires originals or copies certified by the issuing agency. A routine notarized photocopy is not the same as an issuing-agency-certified copy. There are narrow exceptions, including certain overseas military-family applications; do not apply those exceptions to an ordinary adult nonresident case. The IRS also identifies embassy and consular certification options, with availability to be checked in advance. [Source: IRS certified-copy guidance](https://www.irs.gov/tin/itin/itin-supporting-documents).

**Practical example:** an applicant has an expired passport, a current foreign driver's license, and a foreign civil birth certificate. Instead of assuming the expired passport is enough, the applicant can ask whether the license-and-certificate combination meets the rules and which originals or certified copies are required. Renewing the passport is another option, but it is not the only possible document path.

## What a CAA can—and cannot—change

A Certifying Acceptance Agent can assist with the application and authenticate eligible documents within the program's rules. A CAA cannot create a tax reason, make an expired identity document current, or guarantee IRS approval. Confirm that the agent can authenticate your particular document types and handle your applicant category before booking. [Source: Form W-7 instructions, acceptance agents](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iw7).

Other routes include mailing an eligible package or using an IRS Taxpayer Assistance Center that provides the relevant document-authentication service. Availability and which documents can be authenticated matter. Our [CAA, mail, and TAC comparison](/blog/itin-caa-vs-mail-vs-tac) explains the trade-offs without assuming you must mail your passport.

## Prepare these details before asking for help

List the document types you hold, their issuing countries, expiration dates where applicable, whether names match, and whether you have originals or issuing-agency-certified copies. Also identify the federal return or exception that supports your application.

Start with that inventory rather than sending sensitive scans through a general contact form. The preparer can first confirm service fit and the appropriate secure document process. Do not buy assistance on the assumption that it removes the evidence requirements.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I use an expired passport for an ITIN?

Supporting identification must be current when submitted. Consider a current passport or a qualifying alternative-document combination rather than relying on the expired passport.

### Is a foreign driver's license sufficient by itself?

No. The IRS lists a foreign driver's license as identity-only evidence. You also need acceptable foreign-status evidence and must satisfy the remaining application requirements.

### Will any two IDs work?

No. They must be accepted document types, satisfy validity and copy rules, and together establish identity and foreign status. A photo is generally required, with limited dependent exceptions.
