**A non-resident owner of U.S. rental property may need an ITIN when the owner must file Form 1040-NR, claims a refund, makes a section 871(d) election, or has another federal tax purpose and is not eligible for an SSN. Property ownership by itself is not enough; the filing, withholding, and ownership structure determine the need.**

If a qualified adviser has confirmed the federal tax purpose, [start the ITIN application](/itin/apply?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=internal&utm_campaign=itin-rental-property) for Form W-7 preparation and CAA certification.

## Start with the owner, not just the property

An ITIN identifies an individual. A property held directly by a nonresident individual can create an individual filing need. A property held through an LLC or corporation may involve both an entity EIN and an owner ITIN, depending on classification, income, elections, withholding, and return requirements.

Use this identifier map:

| Filing item | Identifier usually involved | Key question |
|---|---|---|
| Form 1040-NR for an individual owner | Owner’s SSN or ITIN | Is the individual required or choosing to file? |
| LLC banking and entity filings | LLC’s EIN | Does the legal entity already have its federal number? |
| Form 5472 for a foreign-owned disregarded LLC | LLC’s EIN; owner details separately | Is the ITIN needed for another individual federal purpose? |
| Property manager withholding records | Owner or entity TIN as applicable | Who legally receives the rent? |

Do not put the LLC’s EIN into an individual ITIN field or assume that obtaining one number solves every filing.

## How U.S. rental income can create an ITIN need

The [IRS guidance for nonresident owners of U.S. real property](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/nonresident-aliens-real-property-located-in-the-us) describes two broad federal treatments. Rental income that is not effectively connected with a U.S. trade or business is generally subject to gross-basis withholding. Alternatively, an eligible nonresident can elect under IRC section 871(d) to treat U.S. real-property income as effectively connected income.

The section 871(d) election can permit deductions attributable to the property and taxation of net income at graduated rates. The election is made with Form 1040-NR and applies to the election year and later years until revoked under the applicable rules. Because that is an individual return, a nonresident filer who is ineligible for an SSN generally needs an ITIN.

This is a tax-treatment decision, not merely an identification-number choice. Obtain advice on the return and election before selecting a W-7 reason.

## Can a landlord apply without attaching Form 1040-NR?

Most new W-7 applicants attach the federal tax return for which the ITIN is required. The [Form W-7 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iw7) include exceptions for defined situations.

Exception 1 can apply to certain passive income—such as rental income—that is subject to third-party withholding or treaty benefits. However, the instructions state that an applicant who is required to file a federal return is not eligible for that exception. The correct route therefore depends on the actual filing duty and evidence, not simply the existence of rental income.

An exception package must identify the precise category and include the listed documentation. A property deed alone does not prove every W-7 requirement.

## Rental-property ITIN document map

The application file can include:

- completed Form W-7 with the correct reason code;
- Form 1040-NR, when required;
- passport or other permitted identity and foreign-status evidence;
- withholding statements such as Form 1042-S, when relevant;
- property-management and payer documentation;
- section 871(d) election statement, when being made with the return;
- exception evidence, if applying without a return; and
- current mailing information for the IRS notice.

## Why use a CAA for a rental-property application?

Nonresident property owners often need their passports for travel and cross-border banking. A CAA can authenticate most qualifying identity documents so the original passport does not need to be mailed to the IRS. The CAA also helps make the W-7 identity information consistent with the tax package.

CAA involvement does not decide whether the section 871(d) election is beneficial, calculate the rental return, or guarantee ITIN approval. Those remain tax and IRS decisions.

## Coordinate the identifiers before filing

Before submitting, confirm who owns the property for federal tax purposes, who receives the rent, which return will be filed, whether withholding occurred, and which EIN or ITIN belongs on each document. If an LLC owns the property, separately review its Form 5472 and state filing obligations.
