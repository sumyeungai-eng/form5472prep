What do filers ask before they pay?

How fast is Form5472 Prep Express filing?▾

Form5472 Prep Express filing ($199) is ready for you to check and sign in under 3 days from your order, 7 days a week, and we fax it to the IRS once you sign. Standard filing ($149) is ready in 5-7 business days. Both tiers get the same package, accountant review and IRS fax delivery.

What's the difference between the two tiers?▾

Only the turnaround. The filing itself is identical: we prepare your Form 5472 + pro forma 1120, a PTIN-holding tax accountant reviews it, we fax it to the IRS Ogden PIN Unit, and email you the timestamped confirmation. Both tiers also include a reasonable-cause letter on late / DIIRSP filings and a filing reminder in the second week of January for next year. Standard filing ($149) is ready in 5-7 business days; express filing ($199) is ready to sign in under 3 days and comes with priority email support.

Is fax filing really included?▾

Yes — every plan includes fax delivery to the IRS Ogden PIN Unit and the fax provider's transmission receipt (destination, timestamp, page count, result): evidence of transmission, not IRS acceptance. You don't need your own fax machine.

What if I'm filing for multiple past years (DIIRSP)?▾

Add $99 per additional past year on either tier. We include a reasonable-cause statement on every late filing so the IRS Delinquent International Information Return Submission Procedure (DIIRSP) is invoked correctly.

Are there any hidden fees?▾

No. The price you see is the price you pay. No setup fee, no monthly subscription, no per-page fax surcharge.
