## 8. Provisional tax mechanics and holdover grounds

Source: https://www.gov.hk/en/residents/taxes/taxfiling/object/provisional.htm (official GovHK page) and https://www.ird.gov.hk/eng/faq/hpt.htm (IRD FAQ)

**Application deadline:** the later of (a) 28 days before the due date for payment of the provisional tax, or (b) 14 days after the date of issue of the demand note.

**Provisional Salaries Tax — statutory holdover grounds:**
1. Taxpayer has become entitled to an allowance not included in computing the provisional tax (e.g. newly-qualifying child or dependent parent allowance).
2. Net chargeable income for the provisional year is, or is likely to be, less than 90% of the net chargeable income for the preceding year (or of the amount estimated).
3. Taxpayer has become entitled to, or to an increase in, deductions (self-education expenses, home loan interest, elderly residential care expenses, MPF/annuity contributions, domestic rent, VHIS premiums, etc.) not included in the provisional assessment.
4. Taxpayer has ceased, or will cease, to derive income chargeable to salaries tax before the end of the year of assessment (e.g. left employment / emigrated).
5. Taxpayer has objected to their salaries tax assessment for the preceding year.

**Provisional Profits Tax — statutory holdover grounds:**
1. Assessable profits for the provisional year are, or are likely to be, less than 90% of the assessable profits for the preceding year (normally requires management accounts covering ≥8 months of the basis period).
2. Taxpayer has been allowed, or is entitled to, a deduction for losses brought forward that was not taken into account in computing the provisional tax.
3. Taxpayer has ceased, or will cease, business before the end of the basis period, or profits for a shortened period are less than the prior year.
4. Taxpayer has elected, or intends to elect, personal assessment, which reduces the amount of tax payable.
5. Taxpayer has objected to the profits tax assessment for the preceding year.

**Provisional Property Tax — statutory holdover grounds:**
1. Assessable value for the provisional year is, or is likely to be, less than 90% of the assessable value for the preceding year (or of the estimated amount), supported by rental/tenancy documentation.
2. Taxpayer has ceased, or will cease, to be the owner of the property before the end of the year of assessment.
3. Taxpayer has elected, or intends to elect, personal assessment, reducing the tax payable.
4. Taxpayer has objected to the property tax assessment for the preceding year.

Note: this list is reconstructed from an IRD FAQ page (which only fully enumerated salaries tax grounds) plus a GovHK summary page covering all three tax types; it is corroborated by secondary sources (Sleek, Fastlane) but I could not locate a single official IRD page enumerating a legally-labeled, fully complete statutory list for profits/property tax holdover in one place — flagged in Confidence Notes. For production use, cross-check against IRO s.63H (salaries), s.63J (profits), s.63K (property tax) directly if the calculator needs statute-level precision.

## Feb 2026 Budget (2026-27 Budget) changes affecting YA 2025/26

Source: https://www.ird.gov.hk/eng/tax/budget.htm and https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202602/25/P2026022500841.htm

1. **One-off tax reduction for YA 2025/26**: 100% reduction, capped at **$3,000 per case**, for profits tax, salaries tax, and tax under personal assessment. NOT applicable to property tax. Applies only to the final assessment for 2025/26 (not to 2025/26 provisional tax); taxpayers must still pay provisional tax as billed, credited later. This is double the 2024/25 cap ($1,500) — a genuine year-over-year change the calculator must implement as a distinct constant per year.
2. **No changes to allowances, deduction caps, or rate bands for YA 2025/26 itself.** All allowance/deduction increases announced in the Feb 2026 Budget (basic allowance $132,000→$145,000, married allowance $264,000→$290,000, child allowance $130,000→$140,000, dependent parent/grandparent 60+ $50,000→$55,000 [55–59: $25,000→$27,500], single parent allowance $132,000→$145,000, elderly residential care expenses $100,000→$110,000, and the extension of the additional child allowance from 1 year to 2 years following childbirth) **take effect only from YA 2026/27 onward** — confirmed by the IRD's own PAM 61(e) table, which places 2024/25 and 2025/26 in the same column, distinct from the "2026/27 and onwards" column.
3. No change found to the progressive salaries-tax bands/rates, the two-tiered standard rate thresholds, property tax rate/allowance, profits tax two-tiered rates, MPF mandatory contribution parameters (rate/thresholds), or the home-loan-interest/domestic-rent mutual exclusivity rule, home loan interest 20-year entitlement period.
4. No property tax rate change and no change to the 20% statutory repairs allowance was found in the 2026-27 Budget.

## Confidence notes (could not fully confirm from an official primary source)

- **Home loan interest 20-year entitlement period**: PAM 61(e) does not restate the "20 years" figure per se (it's a structural rule, not a dollar cap), so this was not independently re-verified against a dated 2025/26-specific official page in this research pass — it is a long-standing IRO provision and no Budget change was found affecting it, but no explicit "20 years, unchanged for 2025/26" sentence was located on an official page during this session.
- **Domestic rent / home loan interest mutual exclusivity rule**: same caveat as above — a structural IRO rule, not itemized as a $ figure in PAM 61(e); not found restated on an official page in this pass, but no Budget change was found either.
- **MPF threshold increase proposal** ($7,100→$10,500 min, $30,000→$40,000 max): sourced only from secondary reporting (Lewis Silkin, HR Online) referencing a 31 March 2026 MPFA/Labour Advisory Board consultation. I could not retrieve a direct MPFA press-release URL confirming these exact proposed figures, and — critically — this is a **proposal, not a change in force** for YA 2024/25 or YA 2025/26, so it should NOT be encoded into the tax engine for those years. Flagging so it isn't mistaken for an enacted change.
- **Provisional tax holdover grounds for profits tax and property tax**: reconstructed from an IRD FAQ (salaries tax only, in full) plus a GovHK summary page and secondary sources (Sleek, Fastlane) for profits/property tax; no single official IRD page was found enumerating all three lists exhaustively with statute citations. Treat the profits-tax and property-tax ground lists above as reliable-but-not-verbatim-statutory; recommend a follow-up check against IRO ss.63H/63J/63K text if statute-level precision is required.
- **Property tax 15% rate source (pam54e.pdf) is dated November 2022** in its footer; I corroborated via a search-index snippet that 15% applies unchanged "2020/21 to 2026/27," but did not find a 2025/26-dated official PDF restating the 15% rate specifically. Confidence is still high (property tax rate has been stable for many years and no Budget change was found) but flagging the source's age.
- All other figures in the tables above trace to https://www.ird.gov.hk/eng/pdf/pam61e.pdf, an official IRD document dated August 2026 (i.e., produced after the Feb 2026 Budget), which is the strongest single source in this research and explicitly separates the "2024/25 and 2025/26" column from "2026/27 and onwards."
