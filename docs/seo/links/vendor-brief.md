## Why
- Google's link spam policy treats links bought or placed to pass ranking credit as spam unless they are marked rel="sponsored" or "nofollow". Unlabeled paid posts with keyword anchors on unrelated sites are the pattern it targets, and it can lead to links being ignored or a manual action.
- Our link report from November shows several earlier placements on unrelated sites with keyword anchors and no label. We would like future ones to avoid that pattern.
- A higher Moz Domain Authority is not a Google signal. Our DA rose because of automatic parameter pages and spun blogs, not editorial links, so we are not using it as a goal.
- AI answer engines tend to cite pages on topic. A few relevant, labelled posts are worth more to us than many unrelated ones.
