# AEO/GEO readiness audit: form5472prep.com (2026-10-05)

**Scope.** I read the repo at `origin/main` `58b8069` using `git show` and `git archive`. The local working tree was not used and nothing in the repo was edited. I also fetched every URL in the live sitemap on 2026-10-05: 259 URLs plus `/form-5472-filing`, all returning 200. For each live page I extracted the JSON-LD, the headings, and the first paragraph under each H2, and I ran each Speakable CSS selector against the live DOM to count what it matches.

**Heuristics used.**
- **Capsule.** The first `<p>` after a content H2 counts as a capsule when it:
  - is 12–60 words long;
  - does not open with a pointer word ("This…", "These…", "Here…", "Everything below…");
  - does not end on a colon;
  - contains at least one concrete anchor (a number, Form 5472, IRS, LLC, DIIRSP and similar).
- **Content H2.** CTA and boilerplate H2s are excluded: "Ready to file?", "Official sources", "Frequently asked questions" and the like.
- **Question H2.** The heading ends in "?".
- **Accuracy.** Blog-template capsule rates are about ±8 points. I spot-read 40+ samples per template to calibrate.

  - Content is the 12 services, 27 landing pages and 181 posts only. **It leaves out the FAQ, pricing, about, press, statistics, compare, tools, EIN and ITIN.**

**Is DIIRSP really Rev. Proc. 2020-29? No.**
- **The IRS DIIRSP page** (fetched with a browser UA, 200, "Last Reviewed or Updated: 19-Apr-2026") **cites no revenue procedure at all.** Its operative text:
  - It applies to taxpayers who "are not under a civil examination or a criminal investigation by the IRS and have not already been contacted by the IRS about the delinquent information returns".
  - Those taxpayers "should file the delinquent information returns through normal filing procedures. Penalties may be assessed in accordance with existing procedures."
  - "During the processing of the delinquent information return, penalties may be assessed without considering the attached reasonable cause statement."
  - Only Forms 3520 and 3520-A get the statement considered before assessment.
- **Rev. Proc. 2020-29 is unrelated.** I fetched `irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-20-29.pdf`. Section 1 says it "modifies the procedures in Rev. Proc. 2020-1 … temporarily to allow for the electronic submission of requests for letter rulings, closing agreements, determination letters, and information letters". It is a COVID-era procedural rule with no connection to DIIRSP.

### Entity signals
