  tigta.gov URL tried (`/sites/default/files/reports/2024-11/201330111fr.pdf`,
  `/reports/audit/...`, the press-release page) redirected to tigta.gov's 404
  page from curl, so it could not be fetched or link-checked. Its findings
  were also about Form 5471 abatements, not Form 5472. Not used.
- **SOI "3,179 reporting corporations" (TY2021)** appeared only in a search
  snippet; not found in the fetched Table 1 file. Not used.
- **IRM 21.8.2 Notice 2022-36 relief**: the IRM text describes it for Form 5471
  modules only, so it was not presented as a Form 5472 figure.
- **Sum of the three IRS burden components** (would be 24 hr 16 min): the
  source does not state a total, so the page gives the components only.
- **State LLC fees**: deliberately not restated; the page links to
  `/llc-annual-fees-by-state`.
