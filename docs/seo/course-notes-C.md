## 7.2 — How to find compact keywords (PhotoAI / "ArtificialPhotos.com" example)

- [NEW] Mindset: target queries where the searcher already knows WHAT they need and just does not know your brand; you are not persuading them they need the product. "Here is the brand that does this; you are already searching for it." For us: "file form 5472 for my foreign-owned LLC", not "what is form 5472". (src: 7.1+7.2.md ~[17:00-19:00])
- [NEW] Three entry points when you start from nothing: (1) a competitor domain in Moz Explore by Site, (2) a seed phrase in Moz Keyword Suggestions then SERP Analysis on the promising ones, (3) ask ChatGPT for bottom-of-funnel ideas. (src: 7.1+7.2.md ~[21:00-22:00], ~[42:00-44:00])
- [NEW] ChatGPT seed prompt (exact shape): "I have an app where people can <what the product does>. What are some keywords I should go after where people are searching for my use case, but they don't know the brand that will satisfy their search intent?" Use it ONLY for starting points, not as the research: you must still verify in the SERP that nobody targets the keyword directly. (src: 7.1+7.2.md ~[42:00-44:00])
- [IN-PLAYBOOK] "Wide open" test: no page in the top results has the keyword in the URL AND page title AND H1 AND first sentence, and no result clearly satisfies the use case. (Playbook: Page Score < ~85.) New detail: he checks all four spots by eye as a no-Moz fallback. (src: 7.1+7.2.md ~[43:00-44:00], ~[29:00-30:00])
- [NEW] No-Moz fallback to Page Score: just check if the keyword is at the start of the page title and in the URL, plus the DA/DR of the ranking sites (Ahrefs calls it Domain Rating). (src: 7.1+7.2.md ~[29:00-30:00])
- [NEW] Judging a candidate SERP: results that are generic tools in an adjacent category (image generators vs "upload photos of yourself" product) mean nobody serves the exact use case -> opportunity. Some low-DA hits (28, 31) in an otherwise high-DA SERP are enough to call it rankable. (src: 7.1+7.2.md ~[22:00-26:00])
- [NEW] Beat-the-top-result method: open the #1 result, ask whether its hero is immediately clear that it does what the searcher wants. If you had to scroll to figure it out, a searcher will bounce. Write a better page: first sentence states exactly what the searcher wants ("<Brand> is an AI photoshoot generator where you can upload photos of yourself to ..."). Google tests your page vs theirs on retention/clicks; better retention overtakes them. (src: 7.1+7.2.md ~[29:00-35:00])
- [NEW] Differentiate from a direct #1 competitor by adding a modifier + "dividing line" in the page title: "<Keyword> | <Benefit/no-signup/free> | <Brand>". Benefit comes from real product conditions ("Free, use now", "no email required"). (src: 7.1+7.2.md ~[31:00-32:00])
- [NEW] Page naming example: URL /uses/ai-holiday-photo, title/H1 "AI Holiday Photo", first sentence "Create AI holiday photos of yourself and those you know ..." (inclusive wording "those you know" so every searcher feels addressed). (src: 7.1+7.2.md ~[37:00-39:00])
- [NEW] Check what a competitor's NICHE PAGE ranks for: run Moz Explore by Site on an exact competitor page (or a small satellite site that ranks for the keyword) to harvest its long tail; 57 keywords on one holiday-cards site all pointed to ONE page. Also see which low-DA sites rank there: they may be doing SEO properly and are worth stealing keywords from. (src: 7.1+7.2.md ~[39:00-42:00])
- [NEW] One page can hit many variant keywords: collect the variants, use them to inform the copy (e.g. generator, photo/photos/portrait, make, family, couple, free, loved ones), but do not keyword stuff. "Use variations of the term." (src: 7.1+7.2.md ~[40:00-42:00])
- [NEW] When ranking one keyword, you usually rank #1 for many similar ones; note that in the keyword notes. (src: 7.1+7.2.md ~[24:00-25:00])
- [NEW] Category 2 changes constantly during research; do not agonise over it, rewrite categories as you learn the niche. (src: 7.1+7.2.md ~[38:00-39:00])
- [NEW] Speed benchmark: each page is written in an afternoon; "in a week you could put up seven of these." Do not slow-walk it. (src: 7.1+7.2.md ~[44:00-45:00])
- [IN-PLAYBOOK] Blank/low volume still worth it ("volume 1" or 0-10 min-max). New: if both a 31-volume and a 1-volume keyword are equally open, take the higher-volume first; in a competitive niche where only 0-10 keywords are open, still take them: they add up and convert well. ## 7.5 — High-leverage keywords

- [IN-PLAYBOOK] Playbook says "prefer high-leverage keywords: reach decision makers, build authority, or earn links". New: Sturm's definition = compact keywords that target more than ONE acquisition: (a) groups/decision makers, (b) brand recognition/CTR lift, (c) authority, (d) backlinks / DA to unlock harder keywords, (e) getting people to DO something for your brand. Prioritize them FIRST, since they are low competition due to tiny volumes. (src: 7.5-7.8.md ~[00:00-03:00])
- [NEW] Group/decision-maker keywords: "<service> for <plural>" -> teams, lawyers, engineers, businesses, schools, districts (one district = tens/hundreds of thousands of users). You can literally put "directors / managers / executives" into the keyword. Decision makers often go by word of mouth but many start with a search, so make sure you are considered. For us: "Form 5472 filing for accountants / CPAs / formation agents / law firms / e-commerce agencies / startup studios" (referral partners), "for founders / directors" etc. (src: 7.5-7.8.md ~[01:00-03:00])
- [NEW] Embed-feature backlink play: build a feature that lets users embed your output on their site, then make a page for "embed X in your blog". Reverb got a #1 featured snippet for "embed audio in your blog"; each embed is a backlink + brand exposure. (src: 7.5-7.8.md ~[03:00-05:00])
- [NEW] Iframe-link nuance: links inside iframe embeds do count for some SEO benefit, especially when users click through; link value follows real clicks. Same logic: links from nofollow social platforms still send positive signals if people click them. Debunks "nofollow = worthless". (src: 7.5-7.8.md ~[03:30-05:00])
- [NEW] SociableKit model: "embed <thing> on your website" pages in the footer + a hub page of embeds; free users show branding with a link, paying users ($60/yr) remove it; either way the vendor wins. For us: a free embeddable "Form 5472 deadline / penalty calculator" or checklist widget with a branded link; paid white-label. (src: 7.5-7.8.md ~[05:00-06:00])
- [NEW] Guest-writer/"article submission" keywords: rank for "<industry> article submission" (or "write for us <industry>"). Professionals with networks want guest posts to boost authority and Googleability; many think any industry blog is authoritative and don't check traffic. Niche down the compact way (biomedical engineering article submission, pedagogy, fintech); fintech was low competition and lucrative. For us: "tax article submission", "CPA guest post", "international tax / cross-border article submission". (src: 7.5-7.8.md ~[06:00-08:00])
- [NEW] Page recipe for an article-submission page: one top image (stock from Pexels/Unsplash), keyword in page title, meta, URL, H1, start of first sentence; 2-sentence intro; CTA button "Submit article" scrolling to a form at the BOTTOM; then ~450 words of copy; no more images; then the form. ## 7.7 — When to deprioritize keywords

- [IN-PLAYBOOK] General advice is "go after as many compact keywords as you can" because each page costs a day. This lesson only ranks them. (src: 7.5-7.8.md ~[15:00-16:00])
- [NEW] Deprioritize when: high DA sites AND high page scores (strong targeting) = competitive. Ideal SERP = low DA and all-orange/yellow Page Scores. (src: 7.5-7.8.md ~[17:00-19:00])
- [NEW] Moz UI moved SERP Analysis to the bottom of the normal Explore by Keyword result; it now shows Page Score, DA, Page Authority, and "linking root domains" (number of DISTINCT sites linking to the page; two links from one site count as one). Use linking root domains as a stronger authority proxy than DA alone. (src: 7.5-7.8.md ~[17:00-18:30])
- [NEW] DA threshold: if SERP Page Scores are mediocre (60-75) but all results are DA 70+, wait until you have DA of at least 30 (maybe 20) before targeting it; Google wants a trustworthy site. This is a refinement to Playbook "DA near yours". (src: 7.5-7.8.md ~[19:00-20:00])
- [NEW] Workaround for high-DA SERPs: make a YouTube video/Short (also IG Reels, TikTok; YouTube ranks fastest) with the keyword at the START of the title and description. "I've done this so many times." (src: 7.5-7.8.md ~[20:00-20:30])
- [IN-PLAYBOOK, detail NEW] Skip SERPs dominated by "Best X" lists (e.g. "5 best team chat apps" with Microsoft on top): not true BOF; list pages need more copy/research; high DA. (src: 7.5-7.8.md ~[20:30-22:30])
- [NEW] Unclear intent (how-to at #1 above a product): relevance 3 not 1; still may be worth building because low-DA sites sit lower in the SERP. Remember that most SEO writers only know TOFU style, so the SERP can be full of TOFU pages that don't satisfy BOF searchers: if the keyword language sounds BOF, a proper BOF page can still rank #1. (src: 7.5-7.8.md ~[22:00-26:00])
- [NEW] Intent-mismatch example: "remote learning" top results = homeschool curriculum, "is it here to stay" TOFU, a definition; deprioritize for customers, but note it can be a high-leverage "we rank for X" brand keyword. (src: 7.5-7.8.md ~[23:30-25:00])
- [NEW] Niche-relevance rule: Google buckets sites by niche; the further a keyword is from your niche, the harder; higher DA lets you stray further. Skip if SERP shows a different product type than yours (e.g. "distance learning programs" shows courses, not software). (src: 7.5-7.8.md ~[26:00-27:00])
- [NEW] Out-of-niche or incumbent-with-keyword-in-domain tactic ("growth hacking newsletter" beat a site with "growthhackers" in the domain): keyword in page title, meta description, first image alt text, body copy, footer link ("growth hacking secrets"); submit to 9-10 relevant directories (Google "<niche> directories") using the keyword in the blurb and link to that page; make a longer page than the incumbent, with concrete examples, to lower bounce and win on engagement.
