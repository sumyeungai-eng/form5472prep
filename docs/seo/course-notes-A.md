## Characteristics of a Compact Keyword ("why compact")

- [IN-PLAYBOOK] Blank/low volume is the signal, not a defect; competition low because people skip them. (src: 1-3 ~[00:15:00]; 2-4 ~[00:01:00])
- [IN-PLAYBOOK] Page length around 415 words; he saw a 215-word page ranking #1 for a BOFU keyword (another cites about 200). (src: 1-3 ~[00:15:00]; 2-4 ~[00:01:00])
- [IN-PLAYBOOK] Templatise and reuse sections across compact-keyword pages. (src: 1-3 ~[00:15:00]-[00:16:00]; 2-4 ~[00:01:00])
- [NEW] Authority claim with a hard number: fresh websites with DA under 10 out of 100 ranking #1-3 for compact keywords. Playbook has DA 8/19-20 examples; this is the generic claim and supports our DA 10 position. (src: 1-3 ~[00:16:00]; 2-4 ~[00:01:00])
- [IN-PLAYBOOK] Ranking #1 for one compact keyword brings many similar compact keywords, so tool volume understates traffic. (src: 1-3 ~[00:17:00]; 2-4 ~[00:02:00])
- [NEW] Mechanism: the target phrase should be the head of a cluster of sibling phrases that all share the same use case; one tightly matched page captures the cluster, so write the page around the use case, not around a phrase string. (src: 2-4 ~[00:02:00], [00:13:00])
- [NEW] Sales-psychology claim: most brands assume a buyer must already know the brand; compact keywords "sidestep" that by catching people who know the need but not the brand. ### Site speed and page speed
- [IN-PLAYBOOK] Slow pages cause bounce, and Google lowers rankings of the page or site. (src: 2-4 ~[01:20:00]-[01:21:00])
- [NEW] His stack: WordPress + Elementor Pro, TinyPNG plugin, Cloudflare CDN, as few plugins as possible because each adds slowness. (src: 2-4 ~[01:21:00]-[01:22:00])
- [NEW] Best-practice list: (1) CDN (geographically distributed cache); flush the CDN cache after changes; (2) compress images before upload (TinyPNG); (3) use WebP, generally smaller; (4) responsive image generation (a 13 MB original is served as a thumbnail; a page with 200+ MB of images still loads fast). (src: 2-4 ~[01:22:00]-[01:25:00])

Instead: test it yourself, ask people in different locations if it feels slow, flush the cache first. A great score can still load slowly in practice. Visitors, not the tool, trigger the ranking penalty (bounce). (src: 2-4 ~[01:25:00]-[01:28:00])

- [NEW] MozBar in-SERP overlay shows PA, DA, link counts and root domains for each result. DA 0-100 predicts ability to rank; root domain definition (one site linking 4 times = 4 links, 1 root domain). (src: 2-4 ~[01:44:00]-[01:45:00])
- [IN-PLAYBOOK] Do not need high DA; example sites under 10-20 DA rank; "voice recording" is a head keyword, "voice recording for students" is the compact keyword. (src: 2-4 ~[01:45:00]-[01:46:00])
- [NEW] Backlink diversity rule: want links from high-DA, mid-DA and low-DA sites; a profile of only high-DA or only one type of site looks spammy. Backlinks raise PA/DA. (src: 2-4 ~[01:46:00])
- [NEW] Trust heuristic: DA as a scam/phishing check (low DA on a site collecting banking or personal data may be a clone, e.g. "Bank of Americas", doubled letters); and a claimed successful big blog with DA under 10 is probably lying. Relevant to us: we collect EIN/ITIN/passport data at DA about 10, so prospects using the same heuristic may hesitate. Counter with visible trust signals. (src: 2-4 ~[01:46:00]-[01:48:00])

## Top 15 NEW items most applicable to a low-DA niche B2B tax-filing service site (ranked)

1. Connect GSC with BOTH a Domain property and URL-prefix properties (https, https://www, any app subdomain), and submit the sitemap index plus each sub-sitemap; list the sitemap in robots.txt. Cheapest fix for crawl/indexation, and the playbook's own finding was that only 5 pages were indexed.
2. Run "Request indexing" in GSC (and the same in Bing Webmaster Tools URL Inspection) for every new AND every updated page. Fast discovery for a low-DA site with few backlinks.
3. Add a free or no-account first step behind the CTA (for example "Do you need to file Form 5472?" checker) so the CTA lands in a working tool; freemium-to-paid is the mechanism behind every example (Reverb, Bitrix24, SociableKit).
4. Indexability audit in Screaming Frog (HTML + 200 + Indexable columns): find accidental noindex, and noindex user-only or junk pages so only intended pages reach the index; protects crawl budget.
5. Run the site-audit spreadsheet workflow: URL, title, meta, H1, H2, depth, word count, Inlinks, purpose, new slug, "delete? (301)", done-after-GSC flag. Catches typos and wrong titles/metas months later.
6. Verify server-side rendering: if a Screaming Frog crawl of a Next.js site shows missing content pages, the problem would also hide the content from Googlebot and AI crawlers.
7. After publishing, test the exact BOFU phrase in ChatGPT ("Recommend ...") and copy the wording customers use on the page; AI echoes site language. Answers vary, so test repeatedly.
8. Use Bing Webmaster Tools as a free backlink checker on competitors (shows backlinks to any site, about 1/4 of Moz's referring domains) to build an outreach list on a tight budget.
9. Win "use case on a big brand's turf" pages (SociableKit outranks Indeed, DA 23 vs Indeed's): build narrow use-case/tutorial pages tied to a well-known entity (IRS, Delaware, Mercury/Wise-type account holders) in a /tutorials or hub subfolder, and add variant pages for each method.
10. Reuse premade section blocks (LeewayHertz) and templated skeleton pages with swapped terms (Zapier pattern, programmatic SEO): for Form 5472, variants such as entity type, owner country, tax year, situation. Keep the intro and H1 unique to avoid thin duplication.
11. Do not chase TOFU "what is Form 5472" blog content or skyscraper pages: the March 2024 update and AI answers cut content-site traffic 50-95%; keep BOFU pages as the sales pages and do not refresh them without cause (stable for years).
12. Backlink mix rule: build links from high, mid and low-DA sites (all-high-DA looks spammy); judge link value by root domains, not total-link counts; remember social/blog links may be nofollow but still help.
13. Seasonality check in GSC: judge each page over its seasonal window (Reverb 400 clicks in February vs 236 in July; our deadline cycle is Apr 15 / Oct 15), and expect Moz volume to understate real clicks.
14. Make trust visible because the DA-as-scam-heuristic exists: with DA about 10 and a form that collects EIN/ITIN/passport data, add named-entity trust signals (company, address, reviews) so low DA is not read as phishing.
15. Treat PageSpeed Insights as a weak signal: do manual, multi-location, cold-cache speed tests; do not lazy-load content that must be crawled; and run the `site:domain "lorem ipsum"` sweep for leftover placeholders.
