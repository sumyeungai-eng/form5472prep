## How to read this

- **Priority** 1 = do first (best effort-to-result: free, indexed, fits the business). 2 = worth doing, lower authority or slower. 3 = weak fit, unclear terms, paid, or unreachable; do last or skip.
- **Link type / DA / DR** are NOT from Moz. They come from the site's own text where it says so, or from third-party submission lists (launchdirectories.com, a public GitHub SaaS-directory list, blastra.io), and are marked "(3rd-party list)". Blank = unknown. Treat them as approximate and re-check in Moz before judging results. Link attributes change; "unverified" means nobody stated it.
- **Live check**: every URL was requested on 2026-10-01. Entries marked "bot-blocked (403/429)" are normal live sites that refuse scripted requests; open them in a browser. Dead or unusable sites were left out (see "Checked and excluded").
- **Cost**: only free or one-time options are listed in the table. Anything paid is flagged in the notes and repeated in "Paid items" below. Skip upsells.
- **Which blurb**: names refer to the copy kit below (25w, 50w, 150w, Tools, Partner, bio).

## Copy rules applied

Approved line used verbatim: "Every filing is reviewed by a qualified accountant before it is submitted". Facts come from the live site (pricing $149/$199/+$99 per extra year, IRS fax delivery, EIN $149, ITIN $349, partner dashboard, free tools) and its About page ("a form-preparation and filing-courier service", not a general tax firm).

## Suggested order of work

1. Same-day free profiles: F6S, Crunchbase, Indie Hackers, G2, Capterra, SourceForge, GoodFirms, SaaSHub.
2. Expert-quote platforms (Featured, Source of Sources, HARO, Qwoted): create the profile once with the bio, then answer 2-3 relevant queries a week.
3. Launch week: pick one tool (penalty calculator), post Show HN and Product Hunt on separate days, then work down the free launch boards (Fazier, Uneed, Smol Launch, TinyLaunch, Tiny Startups, FoundrList, MicroLaunch, StartupBase, Open Launch).
4. Partner/B2B directories only after the owner confirms what the partner program offers (it is per-filing pricing with optional white-label; no commission is advertised).
5. Priority 3 only if time remains.
