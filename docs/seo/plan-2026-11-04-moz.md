### Root causes (ranked)
1. **~10 keywords rank on noindexed `/blog/page/7` and `/blog/page/3`, not on their posts** (thailand #3, outsource prep #5, form 5472 prep #7, colombia #7, coaches #12, brazil #13, owner-becomes #13, indonesia #13, vietnam #14, nigeria #15). Six more (estonia, france, germany, spain, switzerland, recordkeeping) fell out when Google dropped the listing page, and no post replaced them. The posts have a weak crawl path: `/form-5472-by-country` is depth 4, linked only from `/blog`.
2. **Money pages rank for nothing.** The 12 `/services/*` pages, `/compare` and `/form-7004-extension` rank for zero keywords, except dissolved (#3) and late-filing (#9). Google ranks other pages for those terms: "hire someone" goes to a hiring blog post, "compare" to a blog post, "white label" to a blog post, "preparer" to the checker. Those pages don't link to the intended page with the keyword as anchor text.
3. **`/irs-form-5472` carries 8 ranking keywords but has 2 inbound links.** The competitor-name pages (doola, firstbase, clemta, startglobal, zenind, northwest, stripe-atlas) also have 2 each.
4. **Form 7004:** 22 AI answers discuss it and none name us. The page ranks for nothing, and its title lacks "Form 5472 extension", the phrase people search (Firstbase #2).
5. **AI wrong facts:**
   - Gemini says "Express ready within 24 hours". Our pages and llms files are already clean, so the claim comes from third-party pages.
   - Gemini also lists us as "~$99 wizard" under self-service software. Our guides call the intake "the wizard" 47 times and say "we generate everything automatically". That reads as DIY software.
6. **Gap keywords Moz surfaces:**
   - "5472 form" (diff 18, vol 201–500);
   - "form 5472 due date 2026";
   - "where to send form 5472?";
   - "who is required to file form 5472?";
   - "how to fill out form 5472".
