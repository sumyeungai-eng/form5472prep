- **Moz-tracked engines barely mention us, and almost never on category/informational prompts.** Across the 4 engines we are named in 9 of 76 prompt×engine slots (19 data prompts × 4 engines; 11.8%): AI Mode 4, ChatGPT 2, Gemini 2, Perplexity 1. Form5472.online is the strongest non-IRS source (16 citations, 13 of them in Google AI Mode, mostly its own "best form 5472 filing services 2026", "LaunchUSA vs Doola vs Firstbase", "best LLC formation services" posts). Third-party listicles and review sites (GenZone, Entity Inc., Ecommerce Paradise, Hiltzik CPA, Taxhub, form5472.io, Wise, YouTube, Reddit) fill the rest. Our own domain is 13 of 149 (8.7%), and nearly all of it is homepage / pricing / about / terms / security / data-retention pages, not blog posts.

Perplexity "how much does form5472 prep cost?" and "how can accountants outsource form 5472 preparation" cite our homepage and our partner-program blog post but do not name the brand. - **Bing/Copilot is where the content library already works.** 1.3K Copilot citations in Sep 8 – Oct 3 (≈1,278 summed daily; peak 82/day; weekday-heavy), 64 distinct URLs cited, mostly blog/landing pages.
