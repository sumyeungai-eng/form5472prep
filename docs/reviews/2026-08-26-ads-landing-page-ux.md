# Conversion UX Audit — Google Ads Landing Page

**Date:** 2026-08-26
**Pages tested:** `https://www.form5472prep.com/form-5472-filing` (ad landing page) → `https://www.form5472prep.com/start?src=gads` (funnel step 1)
**Viewports tested:** Desktop 1280×900, Mobile 375×812 (with reload for device gates)
**Method:** Live browser testing (Claude Browser pane) — screenshots, DOM text extraction, console/network inspection, click-through of primary CTA. Form was NOT submitted (per instructions), only the first screen was assessed.

## Technical checks

- **Console errors:** none observed on `/form-5472-filing` or `/start` (desktop or mobile).
- **Network requests:** all requests on initial load returned 200 (fonts, CSS, JS chunks, `/api/me`, Vercel Insights, and prefetches for `/`, `/pricing`, `/ein`, `/itin`, `/blog`, `/sign-in`, `/start` variants, `/privacy`). No 404s or failed requests found.
- **Broken links:** none found. All footer/nav links resolved (Pricing, EIN, ITIN, Guide→/blog, Sign in, About, Terms, Privacy, Editorial Policy, Data Retention, Security, Trustpilot).
- **Images:** zero `<img>` elements on the landing page (all iconography is inline SVG/CSS) — so no broken-image risk, but also means there is no team photo, office photo, or product screenshot anywhere, reinforcing the trust-gap findings above.
- **Layout shift / overflow:** no page-level horizontal overflow on mobile (`scrollWidth - clientWidth = 0`); the one contained overflow is the multi-year pricing table (see issue #7).
- **CTA click-through confirmed:** clicking the hero "Start filing — $149" button correctly lands on `https://www.form5472prep.com/start?v=rail&src=gads` — `src=gads` is preserved. (Header nav CTA does not preserve it — issue #4.)

## The `/start` funnel step (assessed, not submitted)

First screen is genuinely low-friction and one of the stronger parts of the funnel:
- Two options: "Continue with Google" or email address only.
- Explicit reassurance: "We'll save your progress as you go and email you a magic-link... from any device," and "We save your progress here — you'll only pay at the end."
- A trust line beneath the form: "Every order is reviewed by a qualified tax accountant before submission to the IRS."
- No password field, no upfront payment, no long form — just email or Google, then "Begin filing →."

Minor gaps on this screen:
- No indication of what comes next (e.g., "Step 1 of 4," or "12 short questions, ~15 minutes") — the 15-minute/12-question framing lives only on the landing page and isn't restated here, so a user who arrived via a bookmark or the header CTA (no context) sees no scope-setting.
- No price restated on this screen — fine for someone coming straight from the pricing card, but the generic header CTA (issue #4) can land someone here with zero pricing context at all.

## Copy problems (beyond the jargon items already ranked)

- The hero paragraph largely repeats the subheading in different words ("Accountant-reviewed, faxed to the IRS, timestamped receipt in your inbox." immediately followed by "Get an accountant-reviewed Form 5472 + pro forma 1120, faxed to the IRS Ogden PIN Unit with a timestamped receipt as proof of filing...") — redundant for a skimming, anxious reader on a small screen; trims well without losing information (see fix #5).
- "WHY IT IS WORTH FAR MORE THAN $149" reads as unsubstantiated hype framing rather than a factual claim — the $25,000 IRC §6038A penalty stat that follows is strong and factual on its own; the all-caps sales-y headline undercuts the otherwise credible, restrained tone used elsewhere on the page.

## Top 5 changes I'd make to lift conversion

1. **Name the accountant.** Replace every instance of "Reviewed by a qualified tax accountant" in the hero/offer/package sections with a named, credentialed person (e.g., "Reviewed by Maria Alvarez, EA" + small headshot). This is the highest-leverage trust fix for a cold $149 purchase from an unfamiliar company.
2. **Surface real social proof above the fold.** Pull the Trustpilot rating/count out of the footer and place a compact "★ 4.8 (XXX reviews) on Trustpilot" badge next to the price card in the hero, plus 2-3 short attributed customer quotes near the pricing tier cards.
3. **Fix the mobile above-the-fold problem.** Compress the hero copy and move a price+CTA strip into the first mobile screen so "$149 → Start filing" is visible without scrolling and without fighting the cookie banner.
4. **Fix the attribution leak.** Append `src=gads` (and any other query params) from the incoming URL to the header nav "Start filing" button, not just the hero CTA — right now a meaningful share of ad clicks that use the always-visible nav button are being reported as non-paid conversions.
5. **Shrink/redesign the cookie banner.** Replace the 3-line, full-width bottom panel with a slim single-action bar so it stops overlapping the Express-tier upsell link and the disclaimer fine print, especially on mobile where it currently eats roughly a third of the screen on first paint.
