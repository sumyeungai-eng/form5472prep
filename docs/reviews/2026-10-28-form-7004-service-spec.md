# Spec: Form 7004 extension filing service, $79 (2026-10-28)

Owner request: "build a page for filing 7004 extension for 79 usd" (+ "use codex to check your work").

## IRS facts (verified 2026-10-28 from irs.gov i5472 / i7004)
- A foreign-owned U.S. disregarded entity (single foreign owner, files pro forma Form 1120 + Form 5472) requests an extension with Form 7004 (Rev. 12/2025): Form 1120 code on Part I line 1 (code 12), and "Foreign-owned U.S. DE" written across the top.
- It must be faxed (855-887-7737, 300 DPI+) or mailed to IRS, 1973 Rulon White Blvd, M/S 6112, Attn: PIN Unit, Ogden, UT 84201 — NOT the regular Form 7004 address. Our fax constant: `IRS_FAX_NUMBER` in src/config/filingPackage.ts.
- It must be filed by the regular due date of the return (calendar year: 15 April of the next year, moved to the next business day when it falls on a weekend/holiday — reuse the existing due-date helper in src/lib/schemas.ts, e.g. filingDueDateUtc). The extension is 6 months (to 15 October). It does not extend time to pay tax (a foreign-owned DE pro forma return has no tax).
- Form 7004 has no signature line.

## Product decisions (orchestrator)
- Price $79 per LLC per tax year → `EXTENSION_PRICE_CENTS = 7900` in src/lib/pricing.ts (single source of truth; no hard-coded "$79" outside copy that reads the constant where practical).
- Pages: landing `/form-7004-extension` (indexable, Service/Offer JSON-LD + FAQ) and order form `/form-7004-extension/apply` (noindex, like /ein/apply).
- Intake fields: LLC name, EIN, LLC US address (street, city, state, ZIP), tax year, contact full name, email (+ optional phone, notes). Tax-year choices = calendar years whose Form 7004 due date has NOT passed and that are ≤ the current year + (allow next year only if needed — simplest: the years Y with today ≤ due date for Y and Y ≤ current calendar year). On 28 Oct 2026 that is 2026 only. If no year is open (e.g. 16–31 Dec edge cases don't exist; just compute), show a clear message.
- Never block payment (repo rule in CLAUDE.md): only email is hard-required; everything else may be blank or imperfect — show friendly warnings; staff follow up after payment. Validation only where a trust boundary needs it (email format, max lengths, PDF-encodable text not required).
- Copy promise: "We prepare Form 7004 (with 'Foreign-owned U.S. DE' and Form 1120 code 12) and fax it to the IRS Ogden PIN Unit, then email you the fax confirmation. Orders are faxed within 2 business days; for a tax year that has not ended yet, we fax it in the first business days after 1 January." Never say "guaranteed", never claim IRS approval; a fax confirmation is transmission evidence, not IRS acceptance. Not a CPA firm. Mention the PTIN wording rule only if the page describes review (docs/reviews/2026-10-17-ptin-credential-copy.md).
- Fulfilment v1 is MANUAL: staff prepare the 7004 and fax it with existing tools; admin marks the order COMPLETED with notes. (Shortcut: no auto-generated 7004 PDF / auto-fax yet — upgrade when volume justifies.)
