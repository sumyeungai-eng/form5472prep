## Facts you MUST verify by curl before stating (cite inline; if a page will not load, omit the claim)
- Processing time: "Allow 7 weeks… 9–11 weeks if it's tax season (January 15 to April 30) or if you applied from overseas" — https://www.irs.gov/individuals/individual-taxpayer-identification-number
- Expiry/renewal: an ITIN not included on a US federal return for three consecutive tax years expires on 31 December of the third year; renew with Form W-7 (renewal box) plus current ID documents; a tax return is NOT required with a renewal unless one is being filed; an ITIN used only on third-party information returns need not be renewed yet. Verify on https://www.irs.gov/tin/itin/how-to-renew-an-itin and https://www.irs.gov/instructions/iw7. Do NOT publish middle-digit expiry schedules unless irs.gov currently states them.
- Notices: **CP565** = ITIN assigned; **CP567** = application rejected (ineligible, invalid documents, or requested information not provided) — reapply with a complete new W-7; documents returned within 60 days of the notice date (https://www.irs.gov/individuals/understanding-your-cp567-notice). **CP566** = the IRS needs more information/documents — verify at https://www.irs.gov/individuals/understanding-your-cp566-notice including any response deadline; if that page does not load, describe CP566 only in general terms.
