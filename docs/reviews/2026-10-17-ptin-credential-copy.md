# Copy rule: PTIN credential for our reviewers (2026-10-17)

Owner confirmed every filing is reviewed by a tax accountant who holds an active IRS Preparer Tax Identification Number (PTIN), and asked to say so across the site and all SEO / AEO / GEO surfaces.

## Approved wording
- Long form (first mention on a page/post): "reviewed by a tax accountant who holds an IRS Preparer Tax Identification Number (PTIN)".
- Short form (later mentions): "a PTIN-holding tax accountant" / "reviewed by a PTIN-holding tax accountant".
- Plain "tax accountant" is fine for further mentions — don't stuff PTIN into every sentence (about 1–3 times per page is enough).

## Never write
- "IRS-approved", "IRS-certified", "IRS-licensed", "licensed by the IRS", "IRS-authorised/authorized", "endorsed by the IRS", "IRS-registered firm". A PTIN is an identification number every paid preparer must have — not an approval, licence or endorsement.
- Any CPA / enrolled agent / attorney claim. Keep existing "we are not a CPA firm / not tax advice" disclaimers unchanged.
- "qualified accountant" without the PTIN qualifier on pages you touch (replace it with the approved forms).

## Scope
SEO (on-page copy, meta descriptions that already mention the review — do NOT change <title>s or URLs), AEO (FAQ answers + FAQPage schema, landing-page Q&A), GEO (llms.txt, Organization/Service JSON-LD descriptions, the site chatbot's facts), and blog posts.
