## Editorial decisions and limits

- **Contractor page contribution:** a payer-side matrix that separates identity documentation, source, Form 5472 relationship, and Forms 1099/1042-S. It does not promise that one form resolves all four tracks.
- **Residency page contribution:** a dated fact-collection checklist and intentionally unresolved hypotheticals. It does not tell readers to stop filing, assign a residency date, select treaty residence, or state a transition-year Form 5472 outcome.
- **CTA fit:** `/start` is used only for a supported Form 5472 package. `/contact` is used for scope questions. Neither article claims that withholding returns, Forms 1099/1042-S, worker classification, residency advice, treaty work, dual-status filings, or personal returns are included.
- **Professional-review limit:** no withholding specialist or residency specialist reviewed these drafts. Claims that would require applying law to a reader's facts were omitted or converted to review triggers. Because no essential unresolved claim remains in the narrowed articles, both are marked `draft: false`; final publication still depends on the parent task's image and repository checks.
- **Maintenance:** recheck Publication 515 and the 1099 instructions annually; recheck Form 5472, W-8, residency, and treaty guidance whenever the relevant IRS revision changes.
