# Form5472 Prep — Growth Plan (written 2026-08-28)

Baseline: ~3–4 orders/week (~180/yr), AOV ~$160 → roughly $29k/yr.
Assets already in place: 90 blog posts, 22 landing pages, 114 indexed URLs, working
conversion tracking, Google Ads (rank-limited), partner portal, abandoned-draft +
January + March reminder crons, Trustpilot profile (~6 reviews).

## The three structural advantages this business has

1. **Filing is MANDATORY and ANNUAL.** Every customer must file again next year.
   Retention is the cheapest revenue available and nobody else is competing for it.
2. **Demand is deadline-driven.** April 15 (Oct 15 with a 7004 extension). ~70% of
   demand lands Jan–Apr. It is now late August — the *build* window, not the harvest.
3. **Customers are manufactured by a handful of companies.** Every foreign founder who
   forms a US LLC becomes a Form 5472 filer ~12 months later. Distribution is
   concentrated in LLC-formation, registered-agent, virtual-mailbox and fintech firms.

Most growth follows from exploiting these three, not from "more ads".

## Tier 1 — highest leverage (start now, Sept–Oct)

### A. Channel partnerships — the biggest single lever
Targets: doola, Firstbase, Clemta, StartGlobal, Zenind, Northwest RA, Stripe Atlas
ecosystem, plus Mercury / Relay / Wise / Payoneer (banking) and virtual-mailbox
providers. Also: bookkeepers and CPAs who refuse international information returns.
Offer: white-label or revenue-share, co-branded landing page, bulk/API intake.
One mid-size formation partner can exceed current total volume.
**Blocker to fix first:** `/partners` publishes no commission terms. Nobody signs up to
an unspecified deal. Publish a concrete offer (e.g. flat $30/filing or 20% recurring),
payout timing, and a one-page PDF for partner ops teams.

### B. Renewal engine — highest ROI engineering work available
The Jan/March reminder crons exist; turn them into a product:
- December/January renewal sequence: "your 2026 Form 5472 is due April 15".
- **One-click renew** that pre-fills last year's entity data (name, EIN, address,
  owner) so a repeat filing takes 2 minutes, not 15.
- Optional "file me every year" standing consent.
At 60% annual retention, year 2 opens with ~110 filings before any new marketing.

### C. Reviews — currently a hard conversion ceiling
~6 Trustpilot reviews for a service where a stranger wires $149 to a US company.
Add the review request to the **fax-delivered email** (not just the portal), 3 days
after confirmation. Target 50+ reviews before January. This lifts ads, organic and
partnerships simultaneously.

### D. Named credentialed reviewer (owner-gated)
Flagged in three separate audits. The site says "a qualified tax accountant" 6+ times
and names nobody. For YMYL tax content this caps both trust and search performance.
Needs: full name, EA/CPA, PTIN, short bio, headshot → then Person schema, /about,
blog bylines, landing page.

## Tier 2 — strong, medium effort (Oct–Dec)

### E. Content: shift from volume to money intent
90 posts is plenty of top-of-funnel. What's missing is commercial-intent and
comparison content:
- "How much does a CPA charge for Form 5472?" (price anchoring)
- "<Formation provider> + Form 5472" pages for each major provider
- "Form 5472 filing service" / "who can file Form 5472 for me"
- Persona pages: Amazon FBA sellers, SaaS founders, dropshippers, consultants
- **Tools that earn links and convert:** penalty calculator, deadline calculator,
  "do I need to file?" 6-question quiz. Tools out-earn articles for links.

### F. YouTube
The audience already watches YouTube for US-LLC-for-non-residents guidance, and
Form 5472 specifically is under-served. One screencast ("How to file Form 5472 for
your US LLC as a non-resident") plus 5–8 short answers to the FAQ set.

### G. Communities where the audience actually is
Reddit (r/entrepreneur, r/ecommerce, country subs), Indie Hackers, Amazon FBA and
dropshipping Facebook groups, Turkish/Indian/Pakistani founder Telegram groups, X.
Answer genuinely; link only when it's the actual answer. This is also how you learn
the real objections.

### H. AI answer engines (GEO)
Already strong (llms.txt, llms-full.txt, schema, AI-crawler allow-list). Keep
publishing the direct-answer format. Periodically ask ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini
"who can file Form 5472 for a foreign-owned LLC" and track whether you're cited.

## Tier 3 — seasonal / opportunistic

- **Google Ads:** currently rank-limited (22.6% IS, 76% lost to rank, 1% to budget).
  Raising budget now buys little. Scale hard Jan–Apr when volume multiplies; add
  competitor and "alternative" campaigns then.
- **Meta ads:** pixel is installed. Weak interest targeting for this niche, but a
  lookalike from the customer list is worth testing in season.
- **Lead magnet:** "Form 5472 deadline + document checklist" PDF for people not ready
  to buy; feeds an email list you can activate in January.
- **Press/citations:** expat-founder and startup publications — doubles as E-E-A-T.

## Seasonal calendar (this is the plan's backbone)

| Window | Mode | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| **Sep–Oct** (now) | BUILD | Partnership outreach, renewal engine, review engine, comparison content, tools, video |
| **Nov–Dec** | WARM UP | Partner deals signed, renewal sequence ready, pre-season content, list building |
| **Jan–Apr 15** | HARVEST | Max ad spend, renewal push, deadline urgency, 7004 extension upsell |
| **Apr 16–Jun** | RESCUE | Late/DIIRSP + penalty-notice messaging for those who missed |
| **Jul–Aug** | BUILD | Catch-up filings, rebuild assets for next cycle |

Doing partnership and renewal work in September is worth several times doing it in
February.

## Instrumentation — do this first, it decides everything else

You cannot allocate budget without knowing what already works.
- `/admin/sources` shows started/paid/revenue per `funnelSource`. Read it and write
  down where the current 3–4 orders/week actually come from.
- Track: repeat rate, LTV, EIN/ITIN attach rate, multi-year attach rate.
- If most orders are organic, the answer is more content + partnerships, not ads.

## Scenario, not a promise

- Retention at 60% → ~110 renewals in year 2
- One mid-size partnership → +100–300 filings/yr
- Content/SEO compounding → +50% organic
- Ads scaled in-season only

Combined, 500–600 filings/yr (~$85–100k) is a reasonable 12-month ambition. The
retention and partnership lines carry most of it; ads are a minor contributor because
the niche's search volume is genuinely small.

## What NOT to do

- Don't buy more ad budget now — you're rank-limited, not budget-limited.
- Don't publish more top-of-funnel blog posts for their own sake; 90 is plenty.
- Don't discount. The $50 promo ended; price is not the objection — trust is.
- Don't chase India/Turkey/Brazil paid traffic again until tracking proves intent.
