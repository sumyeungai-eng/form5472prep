# Competitor / Complementary Provider Facts — LLC Formation & Registered Agent Services

**Research date: 2026-08-28.** All source URLs below were verified with
`curl -s -o /dev/null -w "%{http_code}" -A "Mozilla/5.0 ..." -L <url>` on 2026-08-28 and
returned HTTP 200 (final URL after redirects shown), **except** the three
Northwest Registered Agent URLs, which that site's WAF returns `403` to any
curl/non-browser request (confirmed on the homepage too, so it is not link
rot). Those three were instead confirmed live and loading correctly via a
real browser session (Claude Browser pane) on the same date; treat their
"verified" status as browser-verified, not curl-verified, and flagged as such
below.

This document is facts only, for internal use grounding landing-page copy.
No marketing language. Where a fact could not be confirmed from a provider's
own pages, it is marked **unverified** rather than guessed.

---

## 1. doola

**Offering:** LLC/C-corp formation, registered agent, EIN, bookkeeping
software, e-commerce analytics, banking-partner introductions, and a tax
package. Aimed heavily at non-US founders and e-commerce sellers ("doola is
for Do'ers"); markets to solo founders and small e-commerce businesses.

**5472 in-house? YES.** doola's own blog post states directly: "we prepare
the pro-forma 1120, complete Form 5472, apply every correct transaction code,
and e-file through an IRS-approved channel" (note: Form 5472 for a
foreign-owned disregarded entity actually cannot be e-filed per current IRS
instructions — paper/fax only — so this specific claim on doola's page is
questionable and worth treating cautiously). This filing is bundled into
doola's **Tax and Compliance** plan, listed at **$1,999/yr (discounted to
$1,499/yr)** on their pricing page. The pricing page itself only lists
"Business IRS Tax Filings" as a bullet — it does not itemize Form 5472 by
name or give a standalone price for filing 5472 alone (i.e., you cannot buy
just the 5472 filing from doola without the broader compliance plan).
Evidence URL: https://www.doola.com/blog/learn-how-to-file-form-5472-foreign-owned-llcs/

**States:** No single state exclusively; doola markets formation in all 50
states but pushes Wyoming (low $62/yr fee, privacy) and Delaware as the two
top picks for non-US founders/online businesses.

**Sources:**
- https://www.doola.com/blog/learn-how-to-file-form-5472-foreign-owned-llcs/ — 200
- https://www.doola.com/pricing/ — 200
- https://help.doola.com/should-i-form-in-wyoming-or-delaware-doola-help-center — 200

**Angle:** doola customers on the Starter/Pulse plans (formation + registered
agent only, no compliance package) have NOT paid for 5472 filing — it only
comes with the $1,499–$1,999/yr Tax and Compliance tier. A doola LLC owner
on a cheaper plan needs to hear that formation ≠ federal information-return
compliance, and that doola's own bundled price for the full annual
compliance package is materially more than a standalone 5472 filing service.

## 2. Firstbase

**Offering:** LLC/C-corp formation (primarily Delaware and Wyoming),
registered-agent "Agent Autopilot," premium mail/virtual address
("Mailroom"), accounting, and — as of their "Firstbase Tax Filing"
announcement — federal/state tax filing. Aimed at startups and foreign
founders incorporating in the US; the bundle ("Firstbase One") is positioned
for founders who want formation + compliance + accounting in one place.

**5472 in-house? YES.** Firstbase's own announcement post states the base
Tax Package for LLCs includes "One Form 5472" and "One Pro Forma Form 1120"
for foreign-owned companies. Pricing for tax filing alone is not published —
the announcement says "pricing will vary depending on the forms your
business needs to file" and is determined at checkout. It is included as
part of **Firstbase One**, the bundled plan, listed at **$199/month
(billed yearly at $2,388/yr)**, which bundles Agent Autopilot (registered
agent), Mailroom Premium, Accounting, and Tax Filing together — there is no
separately-priced 5472-only SKU visible on Firstbase's public pages.
Evidence URL: https://www.firstbase.io/blog/announcing-firstbase-tax-filing

**States:** Delaware and Wyoming are the two states Firstbase forms
companies in directly (Wyoming recommended as the default for LLCs;
Delaware recommended for those planning to convert to a C-corp or raise VC
funding). Foreign qualification into other states is offered after initial
formation.

**Sources:**
- https://www.firstbase.io/blog/announcing-firstbase-tax-filing — 200
- https://www.firstbase.io/one — 200
- https://www.firstbase.io/blog/delaware-vs-wyoming-where-to-incorporate-llc — 200

**Angle:** Firstbase's separate educational post on Form 5472
(firstbase.io/blog/form-5472-a-quick-guide) is purely informational and does
NOT claim Firstbase will file it for you — it tells the reader to contact a
tax professional. That means a customer who only bought Firstbase Start
(formation) or an older/legacy Firstbase plan predating "Firstbase Tax
Filing" likely has no 5472 coverage at all, and even current Firstbase One
customers are paying $2,388/yr for a bundle where 5472 is one line item
among many, with no published per-form price.

## 3. Clemta

**Offering:** LLC/C-corp formation (emphasizing Delaware and Wyoming, with
many additional states listed as available), EIN application, registered
agent with US business address, mail receipt, banking support, invoicing,
bookkeeping ("Clemta Books"), and tiered "Federal Tax Filing" as a paid
add-on. Aimed squarely at non-US founders and international entrepreneurs —
Clemta states it has "helped more than 20,000 companies for founders in over
170 countries," and calls out small businesses, e-commerce sellers,
freelancers, and VC-track startups.

**5472 in-house? UNCLEAR.** Clemta's pricing page lists "Federal Tax Filing"
as a feature of the **Pro plan ($89/month)** and Premium plan ($299/month),
but does not name Form 5472 or pro forma 1120 specifically as an included
line item anywhere in the plan comparison. Clemta's own explainer blog post
on the form ("What Is Form 5472?") describes the requirement and mentions
"assistance with Form 5472 filings" as one of their capabilities, but does
not state a price or confirm it is included automatically in a given plan
tier versus billed separately. Evidence URL: https://clemta.com/pricing
(no explicit 5472 line item) and https://clemta.com/blog/what-is-form-5472
(vague "assistance" language, no price).

**States:** Wyoming and Delaware are the two states Clemta prominently
promotes, though its formation page lists dozens of additional US states
(and DC, Puerto Rico, N. Mariana Islands, USVI) as available.

**Sources:**
- https://clemta.com/pricing — 200
- https://clemta.com/blog/what-is-form-5472 — 200
- https://clemta.com/usa-company-registration — 200

**Angle:** Because "Federal Tax Filing" on Clemta's Pro/Premium plans is not
itemized, a Clemta customer cannot tell from the pricing page alone whether
their $89–$299/month subscription actually covers the 5472 + pro forma 1120
package, or only covers a state annual report / other federal filing. That
ambiguity itself is the hook: readers should be told to get written
confirmation from Clemta (or check their invoice/scope-of-work) rather than
assume 5472 is covered, since Clemta's cheapest ($29/mo Essentials) plan
clearly does NOT include any federal tax filing.

## 4. StartGlobal

**Offering:** LLC formation ($399 one-time, state fees included),
registered agent, EIN/business tax number, remote US bank account setup,
virtual US mailing address, US phone number, invoicing/payments,
bookkeeping, "Federal Tax Filing," and state annual reports — sold either
à la carte or via a $149/month "Managed LLC" plan. Explicitly targets
non-US founders: "No U.S. citizenship, residency, or SSN required. We serve
entrepreneurs from 150+ countries."

**5472 in-house? UNCLEAR (leaning no explicit claim).** "Federal Tax Filing"
appears as a service line, priced by revenue (à la carte) or bundled into
the $149/month Managed LLC plan, but Form 5472 and pro forma 1120 are not
named anywhere on StartGlobal's pricing or LLC-formation pages. There is no
evidence-URL confirming they file 5472 specifically — this is a gap in
their public marketing, not a confirmed "no."

**States:** Wyoming and Delaware, with Wyoming positioned as the default for
most international founders (no state income tax, privacy, low annual
fees) and Delaware pitched to those planning to raise venture capital.

**Sources:**
- https://startglobal.co/pricing/ — 200
- https://startglobal.co/llc-formation/ — 200
- https://startglobal.co/us/wyoming/llc-formation/ — 200

**Angle:** StartGlobal sells "Federal Tax Filing" as a generic, unnamed line
item rather than calling out Form 5472/pro forma 1120 by name anywhere in
its public pricing or FAQ copy. A StartGlobal customer paying $99/year à la
carte or $149/month for "Managed LLC" has no public confirmation that their
plan covers the specific $25,000-penalty federal information return —
that's the exact gap a Form 5472-specific service should surface.

## 5. Zenind

**Offering:** Low-cost US company formation ($0 base + state fee, or
$149/$249 one-time tiers with more included), registered agent (all 50
states + DC), BOI report preparation assistance, EIN application
preparation assistance, compliance tracking/annual-report alerts, notary,
foreign qualification, and separate accounting services. Positioned as a
budget/DIY-adjacent formation service for startups, e-commerce, and small
businesses generally (not exclusively non-US founders, though it clearly
serves them too).

**5472 in-house? NO.** Zenind publishes a detailed educational how-to guide
on filing Form 5472 + pro forma 1120, but the guide's own "How Zenind Can
Help" section only claims Zenind helps with "business formation and ongoing
compliance workflows" and keeping "key filing obligations" from "slipping
through the cracks" — it explicitly stops short of saying Zenind will
prepare or file the 5472/1120 package itself, and includes a standard
disclaimer that the content is not tax/legal advice. No 5472 or pro forma
1120 line item appears on Zenind's pricing page either (that page lists
formation tiers, EIN application prep, and BOI report prep only). Evidence
URL: https://www.zenind.com/en-US/help/post/how-to-file-form-5472-and-pro-forma-form-1120-for-a-foreign-owned-single-member-llc

**States:** Registered agent offered in all 50 states + DC; no single state
is singled out as most common in the pages reviewed (unlike doola/Firstbase/
StartGlobal/Clemta, which all push Wyoming/Delaware).

**Sources:**
- https://www.zenind.com/en-US/help/post/how-to-file-form-5472-and-pro-forma-form-1120-for-a-foreign-owned-single-member-llc — 200
- https://www.zenind.com/en-US/pricing — 200
- https://www.zenind.com/en-US — 200

**Angle:** Zenind is the cleanest "no" of the seven — their own detailed
5472 guide, written specifically to educate their customers, never claims
Zenind will file it for them, and it's absent from every pricing tier.
Zenind customers are the most straightforwardly in-market: they've been
formed and told the rule applies to them, but sent nowhere to actually get
it filed.

## 6. Northwest Registered Agent

**Offering:** Registered agent service, LLC/corporation/nonprofit
formation, annual report filing, EIN service, business address, legal
document templates (operating agreements, bylaws), free identity/privacy
tools (domain, website, email, phone number as add-ons). A formation and
compliance-filing company (all 50 states), not a tax-preparation firm; long
track record (founded 1998, Spokane, WA) and consistently reviewed as a
privacy-and-service-quality-focused registered agent, not a full tax firm.

**5472 in-house? NO.** Northwest's own blog post "What to Know About IRS
Form 5472" is purely explanatory (what the form is, who must file, Form
5471 vs. 5472). It ends with: "To determine whether IRS Form 5472 is
necessary for your business, it's a good idea to check with a CPA" — i.e.
Northwest explicitly punts the actual filing determination and, by
implication, the filing itself, to an outside CPA. Nowhere on the page (or
elsewhere reviewed) does Northwest offer to prepare or file Form 5472 or a
pro forma 1120 as a paid service. Evidence URL:
https://www.northwestregisteredagent.com/start-a-business/irs-form-5472

**States:** All 50 states plus DC — Northwest is a nationwide registered
agent and formation filer, not a Delaware/Wyoming specialist; it doesn't
push any particular "best" state for foreign founders in its own materials
the way doola/Firstbase/Clemta/StartGlobal do.

**Sources** (verified live via browser, NOT curl — see header note; the
site's WAF returns 403 to curl/non-browser requests site-wide, including
its own homepage):
- https://www.northwestregisteredagent.com/start-a-business/irs-form-5472 — browser-verified 2026-08-28 (curl: 403, WAF-blocked)
- https://www.northwestregisteredagent.com/registered-agent — browser-verified 2026-08-28 (curl: 403, WAF-blocked)
- https://www.northwestregisteredagent.com/incorporation-service — browser-verified 2026-08-28 (curl: 403, WAF-blocked; redirects to homepage in-browser)

**Angle:** Northwest is a pure formation/registered-agent company that is
explicit about NOT being a tax preparer — their own content sends readers
to "check with a CPA." A Northwest customer has almost certainly received
zero guidance on whether their specific LLC needs to file 5472, let alone
had it filed, since Northwest's product is legal/compliance filings, not
federal tax returns.

## 7. Stripe Atlas

**Offering:** One-time-fee incorporation service. Atlas's own page title is
"Stripe Atlas | Incorporate your startup in Delaware: C corp or LLC" —
**Atlas still forms both a Delaware C-corporation and a Delaware LLC today**
(this is a change worth flagging explicitly: Atlas has NOT dropped LLC
formation as of this research date; both entity types, plus "subsidiary,"
are listed as current options on stripe.com/atlas). Price is a flat **$500
one-time fee** covering Delaware state filing, EIN, incorporation documents,
founder share issuance/cap table setup, 83(b) election filing, and first-
year registered agent; registered agent renews at **$100/year** thereafter.
Includes $2,500 in Stripe product credits and partner discounts. Aimed at
startups (the marketing default is the C-corp), but the LLC option exists
for founders — including non-US founders — who want pass-through tax
treatment instead.

**5472 in-house? NO.** Stripe's own Atlas tax guide discusses the Form 5472
requirement for a foreign-owned single-member LLC directly ("an LLC owned
solely by a non-US individual requires filing Form 5472 with the IRS") but
frames Atlas as an educational resource, stating founders "should
absolutely, positively have a professional tax preparer or accountant
involved" — Atlas does not prepare or file the form itself. Evidence URL:
https://stripe.com/guides/atlas/business-taxes

**States:** Delaware only. Atlas does not offer formation in any other
state; this is explicit both by omission (no other state is mentioned
anywhere in Atlas's own pricing/product pages) and by design (Atlas is a
Delaware-specific incorporation product).

**Sources:**
- https://stripe.com/atlas — 200
- https://stripe.com/guides/atlas/business-taxes — 200
- https://support.stripe.com/questions/getting-started-with-stripe-atlas — 200

**Angle:** This is the one provider on the list where the common assumption
needs correcting both ways: (1) Atlas is NOT C-corp-only — it still forms
Delaware LLCs, so Form 5472 does apply to a real, current segment of Atlas
customers (foreign-owned single-member Delaware LLCs), not some legacy
product; and (2) regardless of entity choice, Atlas explicitly does not
file 5472 for anyone and tells users to hire a professional — so an Atlas
LLC customer is formation-complete but 100% uncovered on the federal
information-return side, with Stripe's own content pointing them
elsewhere.
