   Never throws on blanks; `form7004Warnings()` lists what is missing (name, EIN/not 9 digits, address parts, tax year).
2. **Schema**: `generatedPdfKey`, `generatedAt`, `faxJobId @unique`, `faxStatus`, `faxSentAt`, `faxDeliveredAt`,
   `faxConfirmationKey`, `faxAttempts Int @default(0)` — additive.
3. **Admin page**: "Form 7004" panel (Generate/Regenerate → `<id>_7004.pdf`; View/download; missing-field warnings;

   failed[:reason]`, status back to `IN_REVIEW`, admin email + Telegram, customer nothing, no automatic retry.
5. **Landing**: visible "Last updated" + `dateModified` from one constant.

## Contracts a future editor must respect
- **Fax guards (server, `extensionFaxDecision` in `lib/fax/extension.ts`)** — hard refusals: order CANCELLED or
  PAYMENT_PENDING; no generated PDF; LLC name missing; EIN missing or not 9 digits; tax year missing; tax year not
  ended (now < 1 Jan Y+1, UTC); due date passed (now ≥ due day + 1 day UTC, the `openExtensionTaxYears` convention);
  a send claim (`faxStatus = "submitting"`) younger than 15 min. Needs a written reason (≥ 10 non-space chars, logged
  in the internal notes in the same write that claims the send): already delivered, a fax job with no outcome yet, a
  stale claim, or status COMPLETED / PROCESSING set by hand (may have been faxed manually). A confirmed failure frees
  a re-send without a reason.
- **Never fax a stale form:** any change to a printed field (`EXTENSION_FORM_FIELDS`) clears `generatedPdfKey`; the
  generate route records the key only if the printed fields still equal what it built from; the send claim is pinned
  to `generatedPdfKey`; printed fields cannot be edited while a fax is in flight (409).
- **Exactly what was faxed is what the client gets:** the send snapshots `<id>_7004.pdf` to `<id>_7004_faxed.pdf`
  and faxes that URL; the delivered email attaches the snapshot.
- **Send claim protocol** mirrors `retryFax`: claim pinned on (status, faxJobId, faxStatus, generatedPdfKey); released
  only after a Telnyx 4xx (`TelnyxSubmitRejectedError`) or a snapshot failure; kept after an ambiguous submit.
- **Finalize is exactly-once:** delivered claim `WHERE faxJobId = <job> AND faxDeliveredAt IS NULL`; failed claim pinned
  on the observed non-terminal faxStatus. A re-send resets `faxDeliveredAt`/`faxConfirmationKey` and counts in
  `faxAttempts`.
- **Destination** is `env.telnyx.destination` (default +18558877737 = `IRS_FAX_NUMBER`), the same as filings.
- **Filing fax behaviour unchanged:** the webhook's Filing lookup runs first and the Filing code path is untouched;
  the poll appends an `extensions` summary after the filings (its errors are also pushed to `errors`).
- Copy: delivered email says transmission evidence, "not an IRS acceptance"; deadline moves to 15 October (computed
  with `effectiveDueDateUtc`); never "guaranteed"/"approved". No staff email reaches the client.
