## Contracts a future editor must respect
- **`src/lib/form7004.ts` must not import `schemas.ts`** (it pulls zod + pdf-lib into the client order page:
  310 kB → 113 kB). `extensionDueDateUtc` / `formatExtensionDate` are pinned equal to `filingDueDateUtc` /
  `formatDueDate` for 2018–2045 by `form7004.test.ts`.
- **Tax-year rule** (`openExtensionTaxYears`): years Y ≤ current UTC year whose ORIGINAL due date
  (= `filingDueDateUtc(Y)`, 15 April Y+1 rolled for weekends/DC holidays) has not passed; open through
  the whole due day (+1-day convention of `isYearDelinquent`). Only Y-1 and Y can qualify; the current
  year always does, so the list is never empty. 28 Oct 2026 → [2026]; 10 Apr 2027 → [2026, 2027].
- **Webhook claim for "extension" is keyed on `stripePaymentId: null` only** (no status filter, unlike
  EIN/ITIN) so a payment for an order staff already moved (e.g. CANCELLED) is still recorded and
  alerted. Redeliveries match 0 rows → no duplicate emails; Telegram is retried and deduplicated.
- Statuses (reuse `EinStatus`): PAYMENT_PENDING → IN_REVIEW (paid) → PROCESSING ("Faxing") →
  COMPLETED; CANCELLED. The admin PATCH accepts only these (`EXTENSION_STATUS_LABELS`).
- Copy: never "guaranteed", never IRS approval; a fax confirmation is transmission evidence, not IRS
  acceptance; promise = faxed within 2 business days, or the first business days after 1 January for
  a tax year that has not ended. Prices only from `EXTENSION_PRICE_CENTS`.
- EIN/ITIN behaviour unchanged (branches added only; checkout test pins EIN at 14900 and `/ein/apply`).
