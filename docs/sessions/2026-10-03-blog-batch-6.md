## Why this batch exists in this shape
**Batch 5 (2026-09-24) was lost.** Five finished posts were written in the
`/private/tmp` session scratchpad, never committed, and the scratchpad was wiped before
the next session. They never reached `origin/main` and the branch did not hold them.
The user's memory already carried the durable-worktree rule; it was not followed.

Consequences adopted here and worth keeping:
- Work in `~/Developer/f5472-wt/<batch>`, never the session scratchpad.
- **Commit as each artefact lands**, not at the end. This batch has nine commits: spec,
  draft checkpoint, artwork, then one per revision wave. Writer lanes were told to write
  the full draft to disk before running their own gate.
- Writer lanes were also told the orchestrator owns the scheduler, so a lane's
  `git checkout --` can revert `publishAt`. One did; re-running
  `npm run blog:schedule` gap-filled it back to its original slot.

### A guard this batch discovered the hard way
`src/lib/seo-title.test.ts` asserts, for **every** `content/blog/*.md`, that
`fitTitle(seoTitle ?? title).length <= 60` and `(seoDescription ?? description).length
<= 160`. The batch-6 spec prescribed a frontmatter shape with "nothing else" and
explicitly omitted `seoTitle`; **three separate lanes queried that omission and were
told it was merely optional.** It is not: six of the ten titles ran 64–79 characters and
the suite failed six tests. Fixed by adding `seoTitle` overrides short enough that the
` · Form5472 Prep` suffix still fits (34–43 chars, branded 50–59).
**Contract: any new post whose title exceeds 60 characters needs a `seoTitle`.** Put it
in the next batch spec.

### Audit: 0 P0, 2 P1, 3 P2 — all applied
`docs/reviews/2026-10-03-batch-6-audit.md`.
- P1 — the FBAR comparison post's Form 5472 penalty cell implied a one-off second
  $25,000 and so a $50,000 ceiling. The additional $25,000 runs **per related party for
  each 30-day period** the failure continues after the 90-day notification window, with
  no ceiling. Rewritten using the full clause the records post already carried.
- P1 — its Form 8938 "who files" quote is verbatim from the IRS 8938/FBAR **comparison**
  page while the Authority column cited the Instructions for Form 8938. Attribution split.
- P2 — FBAR threshold quote had elided the source's closing "reported", and the penalty
  quote opened mid-sentence. Both restored to the source wording.
- P2 — § 1.6038A-3 (c)(2)(vi) row relabelled to the regulation's own heading, "loans,
  services, and other **non-sales** transactions".
- P2 — the notice decoder carried a stale "reviewed 11 May 2026"; now "checked 3 October 2026".

**A standing false positive, recorded so nobody "fixes" it:** seven posts say
§ 1.6038A-1 applies "for tax years beginning on or after 1 January 2017 **and ending on
or after 13 December 2017**". That is the Instructions for Form 5472 phrasing verbatim.
The CFR at § 1.6038A-1(n)(1) says "beginning after December 31, 2016", which is
mathematically identical. Do not rewrite one to match the other.
