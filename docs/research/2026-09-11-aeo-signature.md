## Decision and boundaries

Reader: non-U.S. owner preparing or reviewing a foreign-owned U.S. DE's Form 5472/pro forma 1120 package. Decision: separate signing technology, actual document, taxpayer authority, and filing route. Contribution: method/route table and conservative preparation checklist grounded in current IRS sources.

The central publishable answer is deliberately narrower than a universal yes/no: IRS permission to e-file this DE package is expressly absent; neither the reviewed limited-list signature deviation nor generic signature technologies establishes blanket digital-only approval for it. Ink signing of the completed cover, then scanning for the expressly permitted fax route, is an editorial conservative workflow. The special required-fields instruction does not separately resolve signature necessity, so the article does not claim an express pro forma wet-ink mandate, automatic invalidity, penalty or required refiling.

No CPA/EA or attorney review has occurred. No operational signing implementation was changed. Do not present this desk research as a legal opinion or evidence that a specific customer's filing was accepted.
