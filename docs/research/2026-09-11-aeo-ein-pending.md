## Explicit unresolved boundary

The opened special DE Form5472 and Form7004 instructions do not give a complete numberless filing/extension procedure. Article is narrowed to four-situation triage, the actual general SS-4 guidance, the ordinary assigned-EIN extension route, and referral for case-specific handling. It does NOT assert that Applied For is forbidden, universally accepted on every field, or guarantees extension validity. No invented numeric placeholder. This narrow answer is supportable for publication; it is not a substitute for professional sign-off on a pending-EIN filing.
